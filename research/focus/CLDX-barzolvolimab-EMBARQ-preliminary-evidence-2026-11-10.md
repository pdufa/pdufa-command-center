# CLDX barzolvolimab — preliminary EMBARQ-CSU Phase 3 evidence review
Research date 2026-11-10. **NOT_ADJUDICATED; independent specialist challenge NOT_COMPLETED; no numerical approval probability.** Retrospective/outcome-aware data, not a blind forecast.

## Trial identity and FDA stage
- **EMBARQ-CSU1 NCT06445023**, n=963 randomized.
- **EMBARQ-CSU2 NCT06455202**, n=976 randomized.
- Both randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled global Phase 3 studies in adult chronic spontaneous urticaria (CSU) inadequately controlled by H1 antihistamines; total n=1,939. Barzolvolimab anti-KIT mAb arms 150 mg every 4 weeks (300mg load) and 300 mg every 8 weeks (450mg load), versus placebo through week 24. Primary UAS7 change at week 12; placebo participants re-randomized at week 24; active treatment continues to 52 weeks.
- Registries: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06445023 ; https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06455202
- **As of Nov 5, 2026 company update, BLA submission planned in 2027. There is NO FDA-accepted BLA or verified PDUFA action date for these two trials.** Do not invent a calendar PDUFA event. ## Safety and specialty challenges
- Sponsor describes safety through 24 weeks as consistent with Phase 2, but full arm-level serious adverse event, withdrawal, infection, pigment/hair color, hematologic, liver, fertility, mast-cell depletion and long-term KIT blockade tables are required before independent benefit-risk adjudication.
- Evaluate comparative place in therapy versus omalizumab and other advanced treatments, including refractory subpopulation and long-term retreatment.
- Confirm durability at week 52 using controlled vs post-crossover periods; do not conflate open-label/extension observations with randomized placebo comparison.
- Check prespecified patient-reported itch, angioedema, quality of life, steroid rescue, and actual clinically meaningful response thresholds.
