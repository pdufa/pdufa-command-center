# URGN — UTOPIA Phase 3 preliminary evidence review (2026-11-10)

**Status:** PRELIMINARY_RESEARCH_ONLY / NOT_ADJUDICATED / INDEPENDENT_CHALLENGE_NOT_COMPLETED / FDA_PROBABILITY_NOT_CALCULATED.

## Exact FOCUS identity and FDA path
- UroGen Pharma (URGN); **NCT06331299**, **UTOPIA**, investigational **UGN-103 (mitomycin intravesical solution)** for adults with recurrent low-grade intermediate-risk non-muscle-invasive bladder cancer (LG-IR-NMIBC).
- **Phase 3, single-arm, multicenter**, not randomized placebo-controlled; **99 enrolled/treated**, six once-weekly instillations of 75 mg.
- UroGen's **October 19, 2026** announcement says the FDA **accepted the UGN-103 NDA** and assigned **June 14, 2027** as the PDUFA target. This is an action target, **not approval**. The approved UGN-102/ZUSDURI and its ENVISION study are separate; do not import its FDA outcome into UGN-103 prediction.

## Source-reported efficacy and safety (not independently adjudicated)
- At 3 months, **77/99 complete responses = 77.8%** (95% CI **68.3–85.5**); response defined using cystoscopy, for-cause biopsy and urine cytology. Two patients had missing/indeterminate responses; four progressed to high-grade disease.
- Among 3-month responders, **6-month duration-of-response Kaplan–Meier estimate 94.5%** (95% CI **86.1–97.9**). **Conditional on responders**: not 94.5% of all enrolled patients, nor randomized comparative efficacy.
- Treatment-emergent adverse events **59/99 (60%)**, dysuria **19/99 (19%)**; 2 patients experienced 3 serious TEAEs reported as unrelated; no TEAE deaths. Review full investigator tables and attribution.
- **Critical causal limitation:** single-arm design prevents direct placebo-adjusted efficacy. Historical comparison to ENVISION/UGN-102 is supportive context, not randomized equivalence. Investigate formulation bridging, CMC and exposure differences.
