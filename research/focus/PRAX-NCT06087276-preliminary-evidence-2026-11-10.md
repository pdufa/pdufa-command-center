- Sponsor announced positive topline efficacy for Essential3. The full prespecified endpoint, effect size, placebo response, multiplicity, functional significance and missing-data handling need independent protocol/SAP and primary-table verification.

- Review adverse effects on balance, cognition, somnolence, discontinuations and serious events using exact study denominators; do not extrapolate from earlier-stage trials.
