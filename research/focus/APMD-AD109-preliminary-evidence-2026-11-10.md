## Efficacy: estimands must not be conflated
- The company's June 2026 quarter filing reports **treatment-policy** AHI4 reductions at week 26: SynAIRgy 44.1% vs 17.6% placebo, LunAIRo 33.7% vs 7.3% placebo, p<=0.0001. **On-treatment** active-arm reductions were 55.6% and 46.8% respectively; these are different estimands, not directly interchangeable with treatment-policy effects.
- The company reports treatment-policy hypoxic-burden reductions of 44.7% and 37.4%, versus on-treatment reductions of 60.5% and 58.2% for SynAIRgy and LunAIRo respectively. Confirm comparator values, confidence intervals and statistical plan in primary publications/registry before adjudication.
