# SVRA — IMPALA-2 (NCT04544293) preliminary evidence review — 2026-11-10

**Status:** PRELIMINARY_RESEARCH_ONLY / NOT_ADJUDICATED / INDEPENDENT_CHALLENGE_NOT_COMPLETED / FDA_PROBABILITY_NOT_CALCULATED.

## Identity and regulatory facts
- Savara (SVRA); molgramostim inhalation solution (proposed brand MOLBREEVI), inhaled GM-CSF; **autoimmune pulmonary alveolar proteinosis (aPAP)**.
- **IMPALA-2 NCT04544293**, Phase 3 randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial; 48-week randomized treatment, followed by open-label extension. Registry reports ~164 participants.
- Sponsor April 15, 2026 announcement and SEC September 30, 2026 Form 10-Q confirm **FDA-filed BLA under Priority Review** with **PDUFA target November 22, 2026**. This is an FDA action *target*, not an approval outcome.
- FDA extended its original August 22 date by 3 months following a **major amendment** to the BLA consisting of responses to information requests. Sponsor said FDA did not cite safety, efficacy or manufacturing concerns in the extension correspondence; independently corroborate actual FDA documents when accessible.
- As of sponsor September 2026 SEC report, product was investigational and **not approved**.

## Clinical evidence and pending verification
- Sponsor reports the trial met primary **percent-predicted DLCO change at week 24**, with persistence at week 48 and supportive SGRQ quality-of-life and treadmill exercise-capacity measures.
- Peer-reviewed IMPALA-2 report: Trapnell et al., *New England Journal of Medicine* 2025;393:764–773, doi:10.1056/NEJMoa2410542. Independently extract absolute between-arm effect, 95% CI, multiplicity, missingness, rescue whole-lung lavage, subgroup effects and safety before scoring.
- Review distinction between objective gas-exchange surrogate and direct clinical benefit, rare-disease trial power, 48-week durability, baseline disease severity and protocol rescue rules.
- Post-hoc mild diffusion-impairment subgroup analyses presented in October 2026 are **exploratory** and cannot replace prespecified primary endpoints.

## Eight independent specialist reviews — all pending
