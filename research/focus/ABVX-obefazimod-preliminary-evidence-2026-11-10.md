# ABVX / obefazimod: preliminary evidence review (2026-11-10)

**Scope:** NCT05507203 (ABTECT-1) and NCT05507216 (ABTECT-2). This is a *source-backed preliminary analyst note*, NOT a completed eight-dimension dossier, independent specialist review, calibrated FDA probability, or approval prediction.

## Verified-source leads and observations

1. **Induction efficacy (company regulatory filing).** Abivax's March 2026 Form 20-F reports ABTECT-1 clinical remission of 23.8% (38/160) with 25 mg and 21.7% (69/318) with 50 mg versus 2.5% (4/158) placebo, with placebo-adjusted differences of 21.4 and 19.3 percentage points (both p<0.0001). Source: https://abivax.gcs-web.com/static-files/24f1f907-a9ac-4183-88a6-a9bccaf10b84 . These figures are for ABTECT-1, **not** pooled ABTECT-1/2.
2. **Maintenance efficacy (company announcement filed with SEC).** The June 1, 2026 maintenance readout reports Week 44 remission 50.8% (25 mg) and 51.3% (50 mg), versus 10.4% placebo (placebo-adjusted 39.3 and 40.3 percentage points, both p<0.0001). This is a **separate maintenance responder re-randomization cohort (N=580)**, not the eight-week induction primary endpoint for either NCT. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1956827/000119312526252360/d98688dex991.htm
3. **Induction safety (scientific conference abstract).** A January 2026 Journal of Crohn's and Colitis abstract describes integrated treatment-emergent and serious adverse-event analyses from ABTECT-1 and ABTECT-2, with 8-week randomized 2:1:1 dosing. The abstract requires full appraisal of event rates, denominators, duration, and comparator balance before conclusions. Source: https://academic.oup.com/ecco-jcc/article/20/Supplement_1/jjaf231.893/8433392
4. **Subgroups (scientific conference abstract).** A January 2026 abstract reports prior advanced-therapy inadequate-response subgroup analyses across ABTECT-1 and ABTECT-2. Subgroup multiplicity and nominal p-values require caution; no prespecified subgroup success is inferred here. Source: https://academic.oup.com/ecco-jcc/article/20/Supplement_1/jjaf231.1073/8434091
5. **Regulatory context.** Abivax's 2026 company filing described positive pre-NDA interaction and planned submission, **not** FDA approval or a confirmed PDUFA date. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1956827/000195682726000027/abvx-20260630.htm

## Open scientific questions for independent challenge

- Verify each NCT's distinct primary endpoint, statistical analysis plan, multiplicity adjustment, and full induction dataset against official registry/results and protocol.
- Assess baseline UC severity, prior advanced therapy exposure, geographic balance, missingness, rescue medications, and generalizability.
- Separate 8-week induction and 44-week maintenance estimands; investigate selection introduced by responder re-randomization.
- Review adverse-event and discontinuation denominators, duration, exposure-adjusted safety, malignancy and infection events.
- Compare absolute benefits and tolerability against relevant contemporary UC standard-of-care options.
- Review FDA submission status and any regulatory correspondence; no PDUFA date or approval probability is inferred.
