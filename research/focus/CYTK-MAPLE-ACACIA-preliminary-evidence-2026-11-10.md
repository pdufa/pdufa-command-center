## ACACIA-HCM — NCT06081894
- 516 randomized/treated; Phase 3 double-blind placebo-controlled nHCM trial with two primary endpoints at week 36.
- Published efficacy: KCCQ-CSS placebo-adjusted least-squares mean difference **3.0 points** (95% CI 0.5–5.5; p=0.021); peak VO2 difference **0.67 mL/kg/min** (95% CI 0.22–1.11; p=0.003). Both endpoints met nominal statistical significance; independent assessment of multiplicity and clinical meaningfulness remains pending.
- Safety from published report: LVEF <50% in **27/aficamten (10.5%) vs 2/placebo (0.8%)**; serious adverse events **20.2% vs 14.7%**. These must be explicitly incorporated in benefit–risk review rather than treating efficacy p-values as approval certainty.
- Existing U.S. aficamten approval for **obstructive HCM** does **not** establish approval for nHCM. FOCUS records `sNDA filing Q4 2026 planned`, not an assigned nHCM PDUFA date. Verify filing/acceptance with FDA/issuer primary records before changing status.

## Specialist challenge queue (8 dimensions per NCT; none completed)
1. Clinical phenotype and disease biology: obstructive versus nonobstructive cohorts, baseline risk.
2. Design, randomization, comparator and protocol amendments.
3. Estimand, missing data, endpoint hierarchy and multiplicity.
4. Effect magnitude, patient relevance, subgroup heterogeneity.
5. Cardiac safety: systolic dysfunction, LVEF monitoring, heart-failure events and REMS.
6. External validity: enrollment exclusions, concomitant therapies and clinical practice.
7. Regulatory application and indication-specific FDA action evidence.
8. Reproducibility: protocol, supplementary appendix, raw tables and independent challenge.
