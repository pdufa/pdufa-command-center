As of the **November 4, 2026** company update, the nHCM supplemental NDA is **planned for Q4 2026**, not yet documented as accepted with a PDUFA action date. https://ir.cytokinetics.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2026/Cytokinetics-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results-and-Provides-Business-Update/default.aspx

## ACACIA-HCM efficacy
- Company reported ACACIA-HCM met **both dual primary endpoints** at Week 36: Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire Clinical Summary Score (KCCQ-CSS) and peak oxygen uptake (pVO2), statistically significant against placebo. These measure patient-reported symptoms/function and exercise performance, respectively, not mortality or hospitalization. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1061983/000119312526372456/cytk-ex99_1.htm
- Review the published New England Journal of Medicine paper, full supplement, effect sizes, CIs, multiplicity, missing data, baseline therapies and minimally important clinical differences before scientific adjudication.
- **November 9, 2026** sponsor report of secondary ACACIA-HCM analyses: KCCQ Overall Summary Score difference **+4.0 points** at week 36 versus placebo, with responder improvement **33.7% vs 22.5%**, deterioration **7.4% vs 13.3%**; treatment differences dissipated within four weeks after withdrawal. This is KCCQ-OSS, **not** the dual-primary KCCQ-CSS endpoint. Source: https://ir.cytokinetics.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2026/Cytokinetics-Announces-Additional-Data-Related-to-Aficamten-Presented-at-the-Hypertrophic-Cardiomyopathy-Medical-Society-HCMS-Scientific-Sessions-Held-in-Collaboration-with-the-American-Heart-Association-Scientific-Sessions-2026/default.aspx
- November 9 imaging substudy reported reductions in LV mass index and wall thickness (p<0.001); exploratory mechanistic/imaging endpoints are not a substitute for hard clinical outcomes.

## Critical safety and regulatory challenge
- November 2026 10-Q expressly warns that **LVEF below 50% occurred more often on aficamten than placebo** in ACACIA-HCM and some aficamten participants experienced **serious heart-failure adverse events**. These findings must be reconciled against clinical benefit, titration, echocardiographic monitoring and existing REMS. Primary SEC source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1061983/000119312526447408/cytk-20260930.htm
- Prior mavacamten ODYSSEY-HCM trial in nHCM did not meet dual primary endpoints; cross-molecule results are contextual, not direct proof against aficamten. Same SEC source.
- The oHCM MAPLE-HCM label expansion must be evaluated on its own active-comparator efficacy and safety evidence; do not transfer ACACIA-HCM endpoints to MAPLE-HCM.
- Long-term FOREST-HCM is an **open-label oHCM extension**, not controlled ACACIA-HCM nHCM safety evidence.
