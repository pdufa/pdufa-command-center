# ACHV / QUIT cytisinicline — preliminary Phase 3 and regulatory review
As of 2026-11-10. Historical/outcome-aware research; **not** a prospective blind prediction.

## Trial mapping
- ORCA-2: NCT04576949. ORCA-3: NCT05206370. Both Phase 3 smoking-cessation studies.
- ORCA-OL NCT06435221 is a distinct open-label long-term safety study, not a substitute for the randomized pivotal trials.

## Source-grounded evidence
1. **Randomized efficacy:** ORCA-3 enrolled 792 adults, with blinded placebo-controlled 6-week and 12-week cytisinicline regimens, behavioral support, biochemically verified continuous abstinence in the last four treatment weeks, and follow-up to week 24. Peer-reviewed publication was announced April 21, 2025; evaluate the primary manuscript and protocol before scoring. https://ir.achievelifesciences.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/224/achieve-life-sciences-announces-cytisinicline-phase-3-orca-3-trial-publication-on-smoking-cessation-in-jama-internal-medicine
2. **FDA decision:** FDA accepted the NDA in September 2025 with target action June 20, 2026. On June 22, 2026, company reported a Complete Response Letter citing third-party manufacturing cGMP observations and incomplete labeling, explicitly no FDA-identified clinical efficacy or safety deficiencies. This is an **actual nonapproval outcome** for the initial review, not an efficacy failure. https://ir.achievelifesciences.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/238/achieve-life-sciences-announces-fda-acceptance-of-cytisinicline-new-drug-application-for-treatment-of-nicotine-dependence-for-smoking-cessation ; https://ir.achieverx.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/264/achieve-life-sciences-receives-complete-response-letter-from-fda-for-cytisinicline-nda
3. **Long-term follow-up:** October 21, 2026 ORCA-OL report: 479 enrolled, 475 dosed; 69.7% completed 52 weeks; 31.2% four-week abstinence at weeks 49–52. Open-label, uncontrolled, self-selected repeat-attempt population: **not** a randomized effect estimate. https://ir.achieverx.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/274/achieve-life-sciences-presents-52-week-cytisinicline-results-for-smoking-and-vaping-cessation-at-chest-2026
4. **Current resubmission status:** November 10, 2026 company update reports three commercial-scale stability batches, planned NDA resubmission Q4 2026, and *anticipated* possible approval by end of Q2 2027. No verified new FDA acceptance or new PDUFA action date. Company now operates as AchieveRx, ticker QUIT; retain historical ACHV identity in versioned lineage rather than silently rewriting original trial records. https://ir.achieverx.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/277/achieverx-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results-and-provides-business-updates
