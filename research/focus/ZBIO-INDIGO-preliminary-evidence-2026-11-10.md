# ZBIO — INDIGO NCT05662241 preliminary evidence review (2026-11-10)

**Status:** PRELIMINARY_RESEARCH_ONLY / NOT_ADJUDICATED / INDEPENDENT_CHALLENGE_NOT_COMPLETED / FDA_PROBABILITY_NOT_CALCULATED.

## Verified study and FDA pathway
- Zenas BioPharma (ZBIO), **NCT05662241**, Phase 3 **INDIGO**, **obexelimab** in immunoglobulin G4-related disease (IgG4-RD).
- Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled, **194 patients**, 1:1 allocation to 250 mg subcutaneous obexelimab every seven days or placebo for 52 weeks; extension follows.
- FDA **accepted BLA August 2026** and assigned **May 27, 2027 PDUFA target**, reconfirmed in the sponsor's November 5, 2026 corporate update. A target action date is not an approval outcome.

## Source-reported results, not independently adjudicated
- January 5, 2026 sponsor topline: primary time-to-first adjudicated flare requiring rescue therapy, **hazard ratio 0.44** (56% relative hazard reduction), **p=0.0005** vs placebo.
- All four reported key secondary endpoints statistically significant, including investigator-assessed flares, rescue-requiring flare count, complete remission and cumulative rescue treatment.
- Sponsor reports no new safety signal, and lower infections including grade 3 in treatment group; verify actual counts, exposure and confidence intervals before adjudication.
- Relative hazard reduction is **not** an absolute risk reduction. Obtain Kaplan–Meier curves, event counts, absolute flare-free proportions, rescue steroid exposure, competing-risk/censoring analyses, multiplicity hierarchy and full safety tables.
- Mechanism: CD19 and FcγRIIb dual engagement, intended B-cell inhibition without depletion. Check immunoglobulin changes, infections, vaccine responses, treatment interruptions and long-term open-label extension selection bias.

## Eight specialist review tasks — pending
1. IgG4-RD organ phenotype, relapse history and steroid standard of care.
2. Randomization, rescue protocols, blinding and enrollment selection.
3. Flare adjudication, survival estimand, censoring and multiplicity.
4. Absolute relapse prevention, steroid sparing and organ preservation.
5. Immunologic safety, infection, vaccine responses and discontinuation.
6. Organ-specific subgroups, prior therapies and external validity.
7. BLA acceptance, CMC, FDA inspection, PDUFA target and labeling.
8. Independent protocol/SAP reproduction and contrary evidence.
