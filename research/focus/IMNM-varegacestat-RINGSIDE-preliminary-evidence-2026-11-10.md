# IMNM varegacestat (AL102) — RINGSIDE preliminary clinical/FDA review
Research date 2026-11-10. **NOT_ADJUDICATED; independent specialist challenge NOT_COMPLETED; calibrated approval probability NOT_CALCULATED.** Outcome-aware review, not a blind backtest.

## Trial identity and regulatory status
- **FOCUS NCT04871282** is the randomized Phase II/III **RINGSIDE** study of oral gamma-secretase inhibitor varegacestat (AL102) in adults with progressing desmoid tumors. Distinguish Phase II dose-finding (open-label) from pivotal randomized, double-blind placebo-controlled Phase III efficacy.
- Phase III randomized **156** adults, varegacestat 1.2 mg once daily **n=79** vs placebo **n=77**. Primary progression-free survival (PFS) by blinded independent central review (BICR).
- Registry https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04871282
- **FDA accepted NDA in July 2026; PDUFA target action April 28, 2027**, reconfirmed in **Nov 9, 2026** SEC-filed company release. FDA acceptance is not approval. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1472012/000119312526453556/imnm-ex99_1.htm
- EMA validation in October 2026 is a distinct review process, not evidence of FDA outcome.

## Efficacy — pivotal Phase III (sponsor-reported)
- Primary PFS **hazard ratio 0.16**, 95% CI **0.07–0.38**, p<0.0001, versus placebo by BICR. This is a relative time-to-event risk estimate, **not** an 84% absolute risk reduction or an 84% FDA approval probability.
- Median PFS not estimable in active arm vs **24.87 months** placebo in reported analysis. Evaluate follow-up and censoring.
- Confirmed objective response rate per RECIST 1.1 **56% vs 9%**, sponsor-reported; confirm exact numerator, denominators, confidence intervals, independent assessment and multiplicity in full Phase III report.
- Sources: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260530890115/en/Immunome-Announces-Detailed-Phase-3-RINGSIDE-Data-for-Varegacestat-in-Adults-with-Progressing-Desmoid-Tumors-at-the-2026-ASCO-Annual-Meeting ; https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260708283482/en/Immunome-Announces-U.S.-FDA-Acceptance-of-New-Drug-Application-for-Varegacestat-for-the-Treatment-of-Adults-with-Desmoid-Tumors
- Reported pain and quality-of-life effects are clinically important, but verify prespecified scales, missing data, magnitude, timepoints and multiplicity from complete primary publication/ASCO/ESMO abstracts before adjudication.
- Do not conflate Phase II randomized dose selection with the Phase III placebo comparison; a 2026 JCO paper describes Phase II dose finding, not necessarily the pivotal Phase III endpoint: https://ascopubs.org/doi/pdfdirect/10.1200/JCO-26-00725

## Benefit-risk and commercial comparators
- Gamma-secretase inhibitor class has potential diarrhea, fatigue, rash, hepatic abnormalities and ovarian dysfunction/amenorrhea; obtain RINGSIDE arm-specific all-grade/grade >=3 AEs, discontinuations, serious AEs, ovarian function and pregnancy risk before attributing incidence to varegacestat.
- Compare against approved nirogacestat (Ogsiveo), including differences in population, trial entry criteria, endpoint assessment, follow-up and safety. Cross-trial efficacy comparisons are not randomized head-to-head evidence.
- November 2026 SEC filing states Immunome held approximately **$461.6M cash, equivalents and marketable securities as of Sep 30 2026**, with expected runway into 2028; financial runway is distinct from scientific approval likelihood. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1472012/000119312526453556/imnm-ex99_1.htm
