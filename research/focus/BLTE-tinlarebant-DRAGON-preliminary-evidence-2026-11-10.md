# BLTE tinlarebant (LBS-008) — preliminary DRAGON evidence assessment
Research date: 2026-11-10. **NOT_ADJUDICATED; independent specialist challenge NOT_COMPLETED; no numerical FDA probability.** Outcome-aware; not eligible for blind backtesting.

## Trial and population
- FOCUS trial **NCT05244304**, DRAGON, 24-month global Phase 3 randomized double-masked placebo-controlled 2:1 study in adolescent Stargardt disease type 1 (STGD1), age 12–20, n=104 (tinlarebant 69; placebo 35).
- Inclusion in sponsor report: at least one ABCA4 mutation, baseline lesion area no more than three disc areas (7.62 mm²), BCVA 20/200 or better. Verify genetic diagnosis criteria, stratification, missing data, and applicability to older/later-stage patients in protocol and registry.
- Primary sponsor report: https://investors.belitebio.com/news-releases/news-release-details/new-hope-people-living-disease-once-deemed-untreatable-belite
- Registry: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05244304

## Efficacy and estimand
- **Prespecified primary analysis:** definitely decreased autofluorescence (DDAF) retinal lesion growth rate reduced **35.7%** versus placebo, **p=0.0033**, using unstructured covariance MMRM.
- **Post hoc covariance sensitivity analysis**: 35.4% reduction, p<0.0001; do NOT represent the post hoc p value as the prespecified primary result.
- Fellow eye DDAF growth reduction 33.6% (p=0.041). Secondary DAF growth reductions: study eye 33.7% (p=0.027), fellow eye 32.7% (p=0.017). Review multiplicity and hierarchy before calling secondary results confirmatory.
- Company reported little change in best-corrected visual acuity over 24 months in either group. Structural lesion growth is not interchangeable with direct demonstrated improvement in visual function. Examine microperimetry, patient-reported outcomes, functional vision and durability.
- Sponsor August 2026 update: quantitative autofluorescence at month 25 approximately 2% decline/stability on tinlarebant versus approximately 20% increase on placebo; secondary biomarker, not the primary DDAF outcome. https://belitebio.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/belite-bio-reports-unaudited-second-quarter-2026-financial

## Safety
- Sponsor reported **four treatment-related drug discontinuations** and no drug or trial discontinuations attributed to non-ocular AEs. Common ocular effects include xanthopsia, delayed dark adaptation and night vision impairment; headaches among non-ocular AEs.
- Assess functional consequences of impaired night vision in adolescents, ocular AE denominators and severity, dose dependence, RBP4/retinol suppression, vitamin A and long-term safety. Sponsor reported mean RBP4 reduction around 80% with 5mg daily; pharmacodynamic result is not itself clinical efficacy.
- Full adverse-event tables, SAE comparison, exposure-adjusted events and long-term follow-up require independent review.

## Regulatory
- **August 11, 2026:** sponsor announced FDA acceptance of tinlarebant NDA with **Priority Review**, target action **February 12, 2027**. FDA acceptance and Priority Review are not approval. https://investors.belitebio.com/news-releases/news-release-details/belite-bio-announces-us-food-and-drug-administration-acceptance
- Company reports Breakthrough Therapy, Fast Track, Rare Pediatric Disease, and orphan designations; verify precise agency/indication and dates before using them as scoring inputs.
- Separate **DRAGON II (NCT06388083)** and **PHOENIX (NCT05949593)** programs from the current FOCUS NCT; neither is evidence of another FDA action date.

## Independent challenge checklist
1. Obtain full clinical study report/publication and protocol amendments; validate prespecified lesion-growth estimand and masking.
2. Independently audit MMRM model, sample size, missingness, eye selection, and lesion-measurement reproducibility.
3. Determine clinical meaning of structural slowing absent demonstrated visual acuity change.
4. Check safety of sustained retinoid pathway suppression and dark adaptation in adolescents.
5. Confirm population transportability, genetics and FDA acceptability of the primary endpoint.
6. Assess manufacturing, inspection, and labeling uncertainties without assuming favorable outcomes.
