## Critical regulatory history
- FDA issued a **Complete Response Letter in December 2025** for Cushing syndrome. Corcept resubmitted June 17, 2026 with additional analyses requested by FDA. Sponsor primary: https://corcept.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/corcept-resubmits-new-drug-application-relacorilant-treatment/
- The FDA's public CRL reportedly challenged substantial evidence of effectiveness in two Phase 3 studies, highlighting an enriched randomized-withdrawal population, a negative primary hypertension endpoint in the second study, and probable drug-induced liver injury cases. This is **FDA-origin risk**, not an ordinary sponsor uncertainty. Secondary reporting: https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/corcept-shares-tumble-after-fda-letter-reveals-warnings-before-drug-rejection-2026-01-30/ . Obtain and cite the **original FDA CRL** before adjudication.
- As summarized from the CRL by the external source https://www.pdufa.bio/pdufa/CORT-relacorilant : in the GRACE-related randomized withdrawal hypertension cohort, only 46 of 102 with baseline hypertension reached randomized withdrawal, risking enrichment bias. GRADIENT reportedly showed between-arm 24h systolic BP difference **-0.85 mmHg (95% CI -6.7 to 4.9), p=0.77**. These values are **secondary-source leads pending original FDA-document verification**, not independently confirmed trial-specific findings.
- Reported FDA DILI concern: four probable cases, one ALT >50× ULN. Must verify individual case narratives, exposure, baseline hepatic conditions, rechallenge, and causal assessment in original FDA safety review.
