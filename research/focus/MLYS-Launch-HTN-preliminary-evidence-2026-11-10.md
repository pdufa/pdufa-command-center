# MLYS — Launch-HTN preliminary evidence review (2026-11-10)

Status: PRELIMINARY_RESEARCH_ONLY; NOT_ADJUDICATED; INDEPENDENT_CHALLENGE_NOT_COMPLETED; FDA_PROBABILITY_NOT_CALCULATED.

## Exact FOCUS identity and application
- MLYS / Mineralys Therapeutics; NCT06153693 **Launch-HTN** (not Advance-HTN or Transform-HTN); investigational lorundrostat, aldosterone synthase inhibitor.
- Phase 3 randomized double-blind placebo-controlled in uncontrolled/resistant hypertension despite 2–5 background antihypertensive medications. Three arms: placebo, 50 mg once daily, 50 mg with optional escalation to 100 mg at week 6. Primary endpoint automated office systolic BP change at week 6 versus placebo.
- FDA **accepted** NDA for lorundrostat in combination with other antihypertensives and assigned **December 22, 2026** PDUFA target; this is not approval. Source: https://ir.mineralystx.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/93/mineralys-therapeutics-announces-fda-acceptance-of-nda-for

## Preliminary efficacy and evidence separation
- Sponsor Nov 4, 2026: additional Launch-HTN analysis reports 46% of 50-mg recipients achieved SBP <130 mmHg at week 12, >two-thirds ≥10 mmHg SBP reduction. These are descriptive treatment-arm results, **not** placebo-adjusted primary effect sizes. Retrieve original randomized primary results, CI, prespecified estimand and SAP.
- CKD subgroup (sponsor Oct 22, 2026): week-12 placebo-adjusted SBP −9.6 mmHg in CKD (p=.0022) and −12.2 mmHg without CKD (p<.0001). CKD subgroup albuminuria 52% reduction versus placebo; validate subgroup denominators, multiplicity and baseline renal status. These are **not** a renal-outcomes approval claim.
- Transform-HTN (NCT05968430) is a separate open-label extension, not the FOCUS NCT. Sponsor Nov 8 reported −17.8 mmHg from Launch-HTN baseline at week 48 (n=504), with 56% reaching <130. No contemporaneous placebo control: **do not** represent this as randomized 48-week benefit.
- Extension safety: 632 exposed; 27 (4.3%) serious adverse events, 37 (5.9%) discontinued for adverse events; hyperkalemia caused dose modification in 32 (5.1%) and discontinuation in 10 (1.6%). Early serum potassium increases and eGFR declines reported stable later; require full randomized safety tables and renal-risk stratification.

## Eight independent specialist challenges — all pending
1. Resistant vs uncontrolled hypertension definitions, clinical need and background therapy.
2. Randomization, titration, comparator, adherence and endpoint measurement.
3. Week-6 primary estimand, missing data, multiplicity and statistical reproducibility.
4. Placebo-adjusted absolute benefit, week-12 persistence and subgroup credibility.
5. Potassium/eGFR safety, hyperkalemia, adrenal/cortisol selectivity and monitoring.
6. CKD generalizability, drug interactions and long-term cardiovascular outcomes limitations.
7. NDA indication, label, FDA review/inspection and Dec 22 target verification.
8. Independent source challenge, negative data, protocol/SAP and conflicting evidence.
