# CAPR deramiocel / HOPE-3 — preliminary FDA and clinical evidence review
Date: 2026-11-10. **NOT_ADJUDICATED; independent specialist review NOT_COMPLETED; no numerical approval probability.** Outcome-aware; do not include in blind validation.

## Trial identity and study design
- FOCUS trial **NCT05126758**, HOPE-3, randomized double-blind placebo-controlled Phase 3 trial of intravenous deramiocel (CAP-1002), allogeneic cardiosphere-derived cells, in Duchenne muscular dystrophy.
- Reported 106 randomized, 150 million cells every three months over 12 months. Confirm actual ITT, modified ITT, missing outcomes, baseline ambulatory and cardiac status and protocol versions against full clinical study report.
- Registry https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05126758
- FDA July 29 2026 Advisory Committee sponsor briefing (primary evidence): https://www.fda.gov/media/193840/download

## Clinical evidence and analytical risks
- HOPE-3 reportedly achieved its upper-limb function primary endpoint, Performance of Upper Limb 2.0 (PUL v2.0), **ITT p=0.029**, and sponsor reported approximately **54% slowing** of decline. Central MRI LVEF decline reportedly slowed **91%, p=0.041**; these are separate outcomes and their prespecified status, estimands, missingness and multiplicity need scrutiny.
- **Critical methodological challenge:** FDA reviewers publicly questioned post-trial changes in primary analysis, percentage transformation of PUL scores, ranked cardiac outcomes and reliability of the statistical analysis. These concerns are not resolved by sponsor topline p-values. https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/fda-staff-flags-concerns-capricors-duchenne-heart-disease-therapy-2026-07-27/
- The July 29 FDA advisory committee voted **9 to 3 against** the evidence supporting efficacy for DMD cardiomyopathy. This is indication-specific; do not transpose it automatically to a proposed upper-limb preservation indication. https://www.cgtlive.com/view/fda-panel-votes-against-deramiocel-duchenne-cardiomyopathy
- Examine PUL v2.0 raw-scale change, floor/ceiling effects, clinically meaningful difference, steroid exposure, ambulatory status, and baseline cardiac function. Distinguish clinical function from LVEF surrogate and myocardial MRI endpoints.
- 24-month open-label extension is supportive and nonrandomized; do not treat it as a second pivotal controlled trial.
