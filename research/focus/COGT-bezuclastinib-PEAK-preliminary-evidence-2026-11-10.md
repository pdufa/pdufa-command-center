# COGT bezuclastinib + sunitinib — preliminary PEAK clinical/regulatory review
Date: 2026-11-10. **NOT_ADJUDICATED; no independent challenge; no calibrated FDA approval probability.** Outcome-aware research, not eligible for blind validation.

## FOCUS trial and indication
- **NCT05208047**: PEAK, randomized Phase 3 in gastrointestinal stromal tumors (GIST) following imatinib resistance/intolerance; bezuclastinib plus sunitinib versus sunitinib alone. Registry https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05208047
- Distinguish GIST from two separate systemic mastocytosis applications for the same molecule; one company's three PDUFA dates must not collapse into one trial event.

## Efficacy, sponsor-reported
- Median progression-free survival (PFS) **16.5 months combination vs 9.2 months sunitinib alone**, hazard ratio **0.50**, 95% CI **0.39–0.65**, p<0.0001. This is time-to-event PFS, not overall survival or probability of approval.
- Reported in May 28 2026 sponsor NDA acceptance announcement; full ASCO report, censoring tables, imaging assessment, subgroup consistency, subsequent therapies and overall survival maturity require independent review.
- Primary company source: https://investors.cogentbio.com/news-releases/news-release-details/cogent-biosciences-announces-fda-acceptance-new-drug-0
- Compare KIT primary exon 9 vs exon 11 and acquired resistance mutations; distinguish preplanned mutation subgroups from exploratory claims.
- 40-patient **first-line** PEAK extension cohort announced 2026 is a different nonrandomized population and is not evidence for the imatinib-pretreated NDA indication.

## Regulatory, indication-specific
