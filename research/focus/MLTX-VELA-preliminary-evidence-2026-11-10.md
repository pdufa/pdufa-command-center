# MLTX VELA-1 / VELA-2 preliminary evidence — 2026-11-10

Status: PRELIMINARY_RESEARCH_ONLY; NOT_ADJUDICATED; FDA_PROBABILITY_NOT_CALCULATED.

## Exact trial mapping
- VELA-1: NCT06411899. VELA-2: NCT06411379. Both Phase 3, sonelokimab in adult moderate-to-severe hidradenitis suppurativa.
- Both randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled; primary endpoint HiSCR75 at week 16. Combined enrollment 838 (sponsor).
- MoonLake November 4, 2026 report: sonelokimab BLA submitted September 30, 2026 for adults and adolescents >=12; FDA acceptance pending, no confirmed PDUFA date. Do not infer acceptance or approval from submission.

## Preliminary interpretation and challenge
- Sponsor reports week-16 primary endpoint benefit in both studies; higher-than-expected VELA-2 placebo response merits scrutiny.
- **Composite estimand** was the sponsor-designated primary statistical strategy; **treatment-policy** was alternative robustness analysis. Independent SAP and missingness review pending.
- Sponsor pooled week-52 observed treated group (n=396): HiSCR75 67.2%, HiSCR100 33.1%, IHS4-100 26.0%. Trial-specific week-52 HiSCR75: VELA-1 68.3%; VELA-2 66.0%. These are **not randomized placebo-adjusted week-52 effect sizes**; crossover/attrition confound comparison.
- Sponsor reported no new safety signal at week 52. Independently verify adverse-event denominators, infection, Candida, IBD, discontinuation and exposure.
- Pediatric VELA-TEEN data are separate from adult VELA efficacy; do not assume efficacy equivalence without regulatory support.

## Eight pending specialist challenges
1. Disease phenotype and standard of care.
2. Randomization, rescue/crossover and comparator adequacy.
3. Composite vs treatment-policy estimand, missingness and multiplicity.
4. Week-16 placebo-adjusted effect per trial and clinical meaning.
5. Week-52 selection bias and durability.
6. Infection/IBD/Candida safety and discontinuation.
7. BLA receipt vs acceptance, priority review and action date.
8. Protocol/SAP replication and contrary-evidence audit.
