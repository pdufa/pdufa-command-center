# MLTX sonelokimab — VELA-1 / VELA-2 preliminary evidence review
Research date: 2026-11-10. **NOT_ADJUDICATED. Independent scientific challenge NOT_COMPLETED. FDA approval probability NOT_CALCULATED.** Outcome-aware review; not blind backtesting.

## FOCUS identity
- **NCT06411899 = VELA-1**; **NCT06411379 = VELA-2**. Both Phase 3 trials evaluate sonelokimab (IL-17A/F-targeting Nanobody) in moderate-to-severe hidradenitis suppurativa (HS). Registry: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06411899 and https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06411379 ; sponsor pipeline https://moonlaketx.com/pipeline/
- The prespecified Week 16 primary endpoint is **HiSCR75**, a >=75% reduction in abscess/inflammatory nodule count with no increase in abscesses or draining tunnels.

## Pivotal efficacy and statistical challenge
- Company Nov 4 2026 report: VELA-1 primary and key secondary endpoints statistically significant under both prespecified strategies (HiSCR75 placebo-adjusted delta **17 percentage points**, p<0.001).
- **VELA-2 primary composite strategy failed conventional statistical significance**: placebo-adjusted HiSCR75 delta **9 percentage points**, **p=0.053**, attributed by sponsor to intercurrent events and higher-than-expected placebo response. The program reportedly showed positive results with the other prespecified strategy and pooled analyses. Do **not** describe both individual primary analyses as positive or equate pooled significance with both pivotal trials meeting their primary endpoint.
- Sponsor source (Nov 4 2026): https://moonlaketx.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/moonlake-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results-and
- Week 52 **as-observed pooled** sonelokimab participants n=396: HiSCR75 **67.2%**, HiSCR100 **33.1%**, IHS4-100 **26.0%**; VELA-1 HiSCR75 68.3%, VELA-2 66.0%. These are not Week 16 randomized placebo-controlled treatment differences, and as-observed extension-style data have survivor/selection/missingness bias.
