# MLYS — LAUNCH-HTN preliminary Phase 3 evidence review (2026-11-10)

Status: PRELIMINARY_RESEARCH_ONLY / NOT_ADJUDICATED / INDEPENDENT_CHALLENGE_NOT_COMPLETED / FDA_PROBABILITY_NOT_CALCULATED.

## Identity and regulatory evidence
- Exact FOCUS trial: NCT06153693, LAUNCH-HTN, Mineralys Therapeutics, lorundrostat, aldosterone synthase inhibitor, uncontrolled or resistant hypertension.
- Phase 3 randomized double-blind placebo-controlled study in adults on 2–5 background antihypertensives; placebo, 50 mg daily, or 50 mg with conditional titration to 100 mg at week six.
- FDA **accepted NDA March 9, 2026**, assigned **December 22, 2026 PDUFA target** for hypertension with other antihypertensive medicines. Company November 4 update and SEC Q3 filing reaffirm ongoing review; **not yet approved**.
- Prespecified primary endpoint: change in automated office systolic blood pressure at week 6, versus placebo. Do not mistake later week-12 subgroup analyses for the primary estimand.

## Clinical signals and challenges
- Sponsor October 2026 subgroup analysis: placebo-adjusted SBP change at week 12 was −9.6 mmHg for CKD and −12.2 mmHg for non-CKD, with CKD subgroup UACR reduction 52.2% versus placebo. **Secondary/subgroup outcomes**; retrieve denominators, interaction tests, prespecification and multiplicity before scoring.
- Sponsor November 4 update: 46% of 50-mg recipients achieved office SBP <130 mmHg at week 12. A responder fraction without a same-estimand comparator is not a randomized effect size.
- November 8 AHA Transform-HTN **open-label extension**: mean 17.8 mmHg SBP reduction from LAUNCH-HTN baseline at week 48, 56% below 130 mmHg, with 583/632 completing 48 weeks. **Not placebo-adjusted**, and retention/selection must be assessed.
- Aldosterone inhibition raises clinically important hyperkalemia, renal function, hypotension, dose adjustment and interaction questions. Obtain full safety tables and potassium/eGFR monitoring protocols; avoid class-based assumed rates.
- Explore-OSA Phase 2 **failed its apnea-hypopnea index primary endpoint** despite blood-pressure improvement; separate that indication and trial from the accepted hypertension NDA.

## Eight independent specialist challenges — pending
1. Hypertension phenotype, aldosterone biology and clinical positioning.
2. Randomization, background medication stability, titration and adherence.
3. Week-6 primary endpoint estimand, missingness and multiplicity.
4. Absolute placebo-adjusted benefit, ambulatory BP and patient relevance.
5. Potassium, renal safety, hypotension and discontinuation.
6. CKD subgroups, albuminuria, interaction and external validity.
7. NDA acceptance, FDA information requests, manufacturing/label and Dec 22 action.
8. Protocol/SAP, raw tables, contradictory evidence and independent reproduction.
