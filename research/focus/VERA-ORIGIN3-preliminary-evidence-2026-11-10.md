## Source-reported efficacy and safety, requiring independent review
- ORIGIN 3 reportedly met all prespecified endpoints including change in eGFR, annualized slope and composite kidney disease progression through 104 weeks.
- Sponsor Nov 5 report: **annualized eGFR slope −0.6 mL/min/1.73 m²/year** on atacicept vs **−5.6** placebo; **difference +5.0** (p<0.0001). Sponsor also reported **76% reduction in composite kidney progression risk**. Reproduce event counts, hazard ratio, confidence interval and proportional-hazards assumptions before scoring.
- Assess proteinuria surrogate vs clinical kidney-function endpoint, chronic eGFR slope, kidney outcome ascertainment, concomitant optimized supportive care and background RAAS blockade/SGLT2 inhibitors.
- Safety reportedly generally comparable with placebo; verify infections, hypogammaglobulinemia, serious adverse events, withdrawals and treatment exposure from full peer-reviewed tables.
- Published NEJM final analysis reported at ASN Kidney Week 2026; extract protocol, SAP, primary and sensitivity analyses rather than using sponsor percentages alone.

3. Proteinuria and eGFR estimands, multiplicity, missingness and slope model.
4. Kidney-function effect size, composite outcome and clinical meaning.
5. Immune suppression/infection, IgG levels and long-term safety.
6. Baseline eGFR, proteinuria, geographic subgroups and generalizability.
7. Historical accelerated approval vs newly submitted full-approval sBLA, FDA acceptance and pending action date.
8. Independent published-data replication, alternative explanations and contrary evidence.
