## Preliminary interpretation and evidence gaps
- Oral bradykinin B2 receptor antagonist therapy for hereditary angioedema has clinically meaningful acute and prophylactic use cases, but their endpoints and regulatory evidentiary standards differ.
- For acute attack treatment: retrieve time-to-onset-of-symptom-relief definitions, rescue medication, early symptom changes, repeat attacks per participant, censoring and patient-reported measures.
- For prophylaxis: retrieve attack-rate estimand, observation time, baseline attack burden, rescue therapy, missingness and durability.
- Independently verify placebo-controlled absolute effects, multiplicity, sensitivity analyses, and all safety data; do not infer results for one NCT from the other.
- No sponsor headline or BLA/NDA acceptance alone is a calibrated FDA approval probability.

5. Safety, liver function, hypersensitivity, drug interactions and discontinuations.
