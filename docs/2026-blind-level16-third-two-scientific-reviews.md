# 2026 preliminary blind research: third batch (two candidates, L01–L06)

**Scope and independence:** These are desk assessments of the project's previously collected provisional source ledger, not newly authenticated primary-source reviews. They contain no known FDA outcomes, do not create immutable blind locks, and do not establish accuracy. Source dates and event cutoff metadata must be reconciled before any valid prediction. In particular, the ledger marks PDUFA26-TIMING-021 as outcome-exposed; **that event is excluded from this blind research batch**.

## PDUFA26-TIMING-001 — Taletrectinib (Ibtrozi), ROS1-positive NSCLC

**Ledger identity:** NDA219713, supplemental cycle 4; provisional cutoff 2026-08-17. TRUST-I NCT04395677 and TRUST-II NCT04919811. Primary publication ledger date 2025-04-03: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40179330/

| Level | Source-derived preliminary finding | Review gap |
|---|---|---|
| L01 | Drug and ROS1-positive NSCLC indication identified; TRUST-I and TRUST-II are associated with the development program. | Verify that the specific supplemental NDA cycle uses these studies and confirm PDUFA goal date. |
| L02 | Integrated analysis of two multicenter, single-arm, open-label phase 2 studies with independent-review confirmed objective response. | No randomized comparator; assess prespecified cohorts, missingness, and response adjudication. |
| L03 | Ledger reports confirmed objective response rates of 88.8% in 160 prior-TKI-naive patients and 55.8% in 113 previously treated patients. These **are not randomized comparative effects**. | Response duration, CNS activity, confidence intervals, prior-treatment stratification and complete trial reports. |
| L04 | Among 352 treated patients, ledger reports gastrointestinal treatment-emergent events 88%, AST increase 72%, ALT increase 68%, mostly grade 1; 6.5% discontinued due to treatment-emergent events. | Grade 3+ hepatic toxicity, dose modifications, comparator context and long-term harms. |
| L05 | Treatment-naive and prior-TKI-treated ROS1-positive NSCLC groups differ substantially in reported response rates. | Requested supplemental label, patient eligibility, mutation testing and CNS subgroup relevance. |
| L06 | Exact applicability to NDA219713 supplement 4 has **not** been verified in ledger. | Filing/cycle documents, FDA prior concerns, chemistry/manufacturing and goal-date verification. |

**Disposition:** Evidence review incomplete; no APPROVE/CRL prediction, no lock, no scoring.

## PDUFA26-TIMING-016 — Gedatolisib + fulvestrant, HR+/HER2− advanced breast cancer

**Ledger identity:** NDA219908, original cycle 1; provisional cutoff 2026-06-14. VIKTORIA-1 NCT05501886. Primary publication ledger date 2026-03-09: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41802242/

| Level | Source-derived preliminary finding | Review gap |
|---|---|---|
| L01 | VIKTORIA-1 linked to gedatolisib combinations in HR-positive, HER2-negative, PIK3CA-wild-type advanced breast cancer after CDK4/6 inhibitor and aromatase inhibitor therapy. | Verify application-cycle identity, cutoff and whether proposed label includes doublet, triplet or both. |
| L02 | Phase 3 randomized trial with triplet, doublet and fulvestrant arms. | Confirm protocol, allocation, endpoint hierarchy and treatment backbone. |
| L03 | Ledger reports median blinded independent-review PFS of 9.3 months (triplet), 7.4 (doublet), 2.0 (fulvestrant); HR versus fulvestrant 0.24 (95% CI 0.17–0.35) for triplet and 0.33 (0.24–0.48) for doublet. | Multiplicity, OS maturity, censoring, subgroup effects and prespecified statistical thresholds. |
| L04 | Ledger reports grade ≥3 treatment-related neutropenia 62.3% with triplet versus 0.8% with doublet; stomatitis 19.2% versus 12.3%. This is a potentially material regimen-specific toxicity difference. | Full safety by arm, dose reductions, supportive care, discontinuations and patient-reported outcomes. |
| L05 | Trial population restricted to PIK3CA-wild-type advanced breast cancer after specified prior therapies. | Verify proposed indication and whether evidence supports each regimen separately. |
| L06 | Publication of positive PFS findings does not establish approval readiness. | Confirm NDA and goal date, complete submission, CMC and FDA concerns. |

**Disposition:** Evidence review incomplete; no APPROVE/CRL prediction, no lock, no scoring.
