## UPDATE — 2026-11-19: SOURCE RETRIEVAL SUCCESSFUL

**This update supersedes the earlier SOURCE_AUTHENTICATION_BLOCKED result for the ORCA-2 journal abstract only.** PubMed PMID 37432430 is now directly accessible and the original JAMA article's abstract, publication date and trial registration have been checked: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37432430/ (JAMA 2023 Jul 11;330(2):152–160; DOI 10.1001/jama.2023.10042; NCT04576949). This is a pre-cutoff publication.

**Independently confirmed from PubMed:** randomized, three-group, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial at 17 US sites; 810 adults, randomized to 12-week cytisinicline (n=270), 6-week cytisinicline then placebo (n=269), or placebo (n=271), all with behavioral support. The 12-week treatment's biochemically verified continuous abstinence in weeks 9–12 was 32.6% versus 7.0% (OR 6.3, 95% CI 3.7–11.6, P<0.001). Sustained abstinence in weeks 9–24 was 21.1% versus 4.8% (OR 5.3, 95% CI 2.8–11.1, P<0.001). The six-week arm's weeks 3–6 abstinence was 25.3% versus 4.4% (OR 8.0, 95% CI 3.9–16.3, P<0.001). Sixteen cytisinicline recipients (2.9%) discontinued because of adverse events, and the abstract reports no drug-related serious adverse events.

**Verified scope:** Original journal abstract/date, study registration and the figures above. **Still unverified:** full protocol and article tables, companion pivotal trial, NDA218995 and precise original review-cycle linkage, source-version archive, and outcome-unexposed reviewer attestation. This publication authentication does **not** authorize a directional FDA prediction, prediction lock, or accuracy score. Existing preliminary narrative coverage remains 22/89 candidates and 132 level narratives.

## UPDATE — 2026-11-19: COMPANION PIVOTAL ORCA-3 VERIFIED

The second pivotal smoking-cessation trial is now identified and independently checked against its original 2025 journal article, PubMed and PMC: **ORCA-3**, NCT05206370, Rigotti et al., *JAMA Internal Medicine* 2025;185(6):648–655, DOI **10.1001/jamainternmed.2025.0628**, published online **2025-04-21**. Sources: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40257755/ ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12012700/ . The publication predates the provisional 2026-05-21 research cutoff.

**Verified study design:** Phase 3, three-arm, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled replication trial at 20 US sites; **792 randomized** (264 assigned 12-week cytisinicline, 263 assigned 6-week cytisinicline then placebo, 265 placebo), with behavioral support. NCT05206370 is **distinct from ORCA-2 NCT04576949**.

**Verified efficacy:** 12-week regimen weeks 9–12 continuous abstinence **30.3% versus 9.4%** placebo, OR **4.4** (95% CI **2.6–7.3**), P<0.001. Weeks 9–24 continuous abstinence **20.5% versus 4.2%**. Six-week regimen weeks 3–6 abstinence **14.8% versus 6.0%**, OR **2.9** (95% CI **1.5–5.6**), P<0.001. These are placebo-controlled smoking-abstinence results; they are **not** direct evidence of superiority to varenicline or other active medications.

**Verified safety summary:** Abstract reports no treatment-related serious adverse events. More detailed safety tables, psychiatric and cardiovascular exclusions, treatment discontinuations and 24-week follow-up must be evaluated before a complete scientific judgment. Trial eligibility excluded several high-risk clinical groups; generalization needs care.

**Evidence status after this update:** **Both ORCA-2 and ORCA-3 original journal publications and online dates have been authenticated.** The companion-trial identification blocker from the prior update is resolved. **Still outstanding:** independent FDA NDA218995/review-cycle/goal-date verification, exact trial-to-submission mapping, protocol/SAP and complete safety review, independent reviewer outcome-nonexposure attestation, immutable evidence manifest and blind prediction lock. **Do not issue a directional FDA prediction or compute accuracy yet.** Coverage accounting stays at 22/89 provisional candidate reviews and 132 provisional level narratives.
