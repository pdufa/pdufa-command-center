Research note prepared 2026-10-28. Historical cutoff: 2025-08-15.
**Not an out-of-sample forecast. No calibrated approval probability.**

## Predecision evidence

1. SEC-filed June 10, 2024 presentation:
   https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1430306/000183988224018368/ex99-02.htm
   - RELIEF: positive Phase 3, reported December 2020.
   - RALLY: missed primary endpoint, reported July 2021.
   - RESILIENT: positive confirmatory Phase 3, reported December 2023.
   - RESILIENT primary pain diary p=0.00005.
   - Week-14 change -1.82 active vs -1.16 placebo;
     placebo-adjusted difference approximately -0.65, SE 0.16.
   - Sponsor's presentation: **sponsor-reported**, not independently

3. Peer-reviewed 2024 RESILIENT abstract:
   https://ard.bmj.com/content/83/Suppl_1/321
   - Contemporary external publication, but authors include sponsor

## Critical scientific assessment — preliminary

**Efficacy:** Two pivotal trials reported statistically significant
pain improvement, while RALLY failed its primary endpoint. This is
meaningful replication evidence but not proof of approvability.
**Clinical magnitude:** RESILIENT mean between-arm difference ~0.65
points on the reported pain scale; responder distribution and
patient-important thresholds need separate review.
**Consistency:** RALLY failure requires assessment of protocol,
pandemic-era dropout, site effects, missingness, placebo response,
and whether explanations were prespecified.
**Multiplicity:** RESILIENT secondary outcomes used a reported serial
gatekeeping procedure; verify analysis plan before certifying claims.
**Safety:** Evaluate oral/sublingual local reactions, anticholinergic
effects, sedation, drug interactions, and discontinuations across
trials. A sponsor-reported safety summary is insufficient.
**Regulatory:** NDA submission and Fast Track status do not guarantee
approval. CMC/inspection, labeling, and FDA review issues cannot be
inferred resolved from efficacy alone.
