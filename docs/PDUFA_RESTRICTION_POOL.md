## FDA special designations and programs

All of the FDA statuses discussed are represented in the pool. The existing combined entries retain their original names and identities.

| Status or program | Type | Pool entry | Update |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fast Track | FDA designation | Fast Track (`alert_fast_track`) | Added |
| Breakthrough Therapy | FDA designation | Breakthrough Therapy / RMAT (`alert_breakthrough_rmat`) | Existing combined entry retained |
| Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy (RMAT) | FDA designation | Breakthrough Therapy / RMAT (`alert_breakthrough_rmat`) | Existing combined entry retained |
| Orphan Drug | FDA designation | Unmet Medical Need / Orphan Status (`alert_orphan_unmet`) | Existing combined entry retained |
| Priority Review | FDA review designation | Priority Review (`alert_priority_review`) | Existing entry retained |
| Accelerated Approval | FDA approval pathway | Accelerated Approval (`alert_accelerated_approval`) | Added |

Fast Track and Accelerated Approval appear under **3. FDA & REGULATORY STATUS**. They start optional, disabled, and unassigned to a list. All original Pipeline, GEM JR, GEM, FS, and FA associations, thresholds, and saved priorities are retained.

Pool membership records an available choice; it does not establish that any trial has that status. The optional regulatory goals currently return **UNKNOWN** until evidence-based evaluation is connected. A requested designation or proposed accelerated-approval strategy does not establish a granted status or approval.
