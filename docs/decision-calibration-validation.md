# Historical prediction calibration: research diagnostics

`new-platform/frontend/lib/decision-calibration-holdout.ts` evaluates a declared chronological holdout. It does not fit a probability model, certify source documents, or validate prediction accuracy. It always returns `tradeAuthorized: false` and `validatedPredictionAccuracy: false`.

Supply the original frozen model version and HTTPS plan locator, freeze date, holdout forecast window, evaluation cutoff, and exact training/holdout program membership. Each forecast needs the existing historical evidence fields plus its canonical FDA application number (`NDA` or `BLA` followed by six digits), model version and subgroup. A later reviewer must independently verify the underlying records and that the plan and membership were recorded before the holdout began.

The evaluator requires at least 30 cases in each partition. Training forecasts and observed outcomes must precede the freeze; holdout forecasts must fall within the declared window. Prediction capture must strictly precede the FDA decision, and holdout outcome observations must precede the evaluation cutoff. An FDA application must belong entirely to one partition, even when different program IDs identify separate indications or review cycles. Multiple training cycles for one application are permitted only with otherwise distinct program IDs; they do not become independent applications or establish an independent sample size. Missing or unexpected cases, overlapping identities or applications, model changes, missing source/review metadata, and invalid dates withhold all metrics. It never silently drops incomplete cases.

Outputs include holdout Brier score, log loss, a reliability table suitable for a calibration plot, expected calibration error, and subgroup diagnostics. Underpowered subgroups have no scores. The comparison baseline is the approval frequency in training, frozen before holdout observation; holdout prevalence is not used to fit the benchmark. The older `summarizeHistoricalCalibration` function remains a descriptive cohort summary whose same-cohort baseline must not be treated as an out-of-time benchmark.

Synthetic regression fixtures verify arithmetic, membership, and leakage handling only. No independently verified historical forecast cohort is supplied with this change, so no real model performance or prediction accuracy has been established. Completing validation still requires original predecision forecasts, independently reviewed FDA application/indication/review-cycle outcome evidence, a complete approval/nonapproval denominator including delisted issuers, independent forward evaluation, and review of model/threshold selection. Trading strategy performance additionally needs verified market data, transaction costs, and portfolio risk evaluation.
