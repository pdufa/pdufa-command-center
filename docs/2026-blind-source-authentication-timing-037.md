## Authenticated publication findings, not prediction

- **Design:** 52-week, open-label, phase 1/2 trial; 32 adults with HPV-6/11-associated RRP who needed at least two surgical interventions in the preceding year. Four intramuscular INO-3107 doses with electroporation.
- **Primary endpoint:** safety and tolerability. Surgery frequency was a secondary endpoint.
- **Clinical signal:** 26/32 (81%) had fewer surgeries during follow-up than in the preceding year. This is a historical within-patient comparison, **not** a randomized estimate of benefit over standard care.
- **Safety:** treatment-related adverse events in 13/32 (41%), described as low-grade in the abstract.
- **Mechanistic observations:** antigen-specific T-cell induction and tissue immune-response changes; these do not establish regulatory efficacy on their own.
