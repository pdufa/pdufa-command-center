## RALLY (F306): failed primary endpoint

- Sponsor released results on 2022-03-21.
- 514 randomized, 36 US sites, 14-week double-blind placebo-controlled.
- Pain change at week 14: active -1.6 versus placebo -1.3;
  adjusted between-arm difference approximately -0.2 (SE 0.16),
  p=0.115. Primary endpoint FAILED.
- Completion: active 73.8%, placebo 81.4%.
- Adverse-event-related early discontinuation: active 15.2%,
  placebo 6.2%. Versus RELIEF, corresponding rates were
  active 8.5%, placebo 3.5%.
- Company stated that multiple imputation of missing data attenuated
  efficacy significance; this is a sponsor explanation, NOT an
  independently established causal account of failure.
- Nominal PGIC responder difference +8.0 percentage points,
  p=0.038; nominal sleep-disturbance endpoint p=0.004.
  Neither rescues the failed prespecified primary endpoint.
- First interim cohort enrolled September 2020–March 2021,
  overlapping COVID-19 pandemic waves. A pandemic explanation
  remains a hypothesis without participant-level sensitivity analysis.
- Independent monitoring committee recommended stopping new
  enrollment for futility in July 2021; already enrolled participants
  were followed.

## Critical review and prediction constraints

A higher attrition rate can bias endpoint estimates and make the
missing-data estimand crucial. Needed before certifying any explanation:
full protocol and amendments, SAP with imputation assumptions,
patient-level dropout reasons by arm and time, sensitivity analyses
(MNAR/tipping point), site/time subgroup diagnostics, baseline
balance, exposure/adherence, treatment-emergent adverse events, and
independent CSR verification. Compare with RELIEF and RESILIENT on
endpoint definitions, dose, recruitment, missingness and multiplicity.

Predecision interpretation: RALLY provides a meaningful negative
replication signal; the sponsor's attrition explanation is plausible
but unproven. Positive RELIEF and RESILIENT evidence must be evaluated
alongside, not substituted for, this failed trial.
