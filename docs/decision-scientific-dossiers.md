# Scientific investigation dossiers and current readiness

SCIENCE now has a source-cited dossier contract for one exact program/trial/ticker/stage: 20 investigation levels, two assessments per level (40 total), and eight distinct scientific subject layers. Layer names and level definitions come from the reviewed scientific protocol supplied with the dossier; the software does not invent a medical taxonomy or assign scientific scores. Each assessment records its original citations, publication and observation timestamps, reviewer, critical evaluation, uncertainty and unresolved critical findings.

Every declared assessment needs a complete review record, independent source-review attestation, original HTTPS citation available before the dossier cutoff, critical evaluation and uncertainty text, and no unresolved critical finding. Missing/duplicate levels or assessments, missing layers, conflicts, credential-bearing URLs, impossible/ambiguous timestamps and identity mismatches block readiness. A seven-category score or an asserted `reviewedLevels: 20` summary cannot substitute for the underlying 40 records. A supplied summary must reconcile to the source-cited inventory.

The audit reports review-record coverage, not independently established truth. `readyForAnalysis` can reach only `READY_FOR_RESEARCH_REVIEW`; the separate reluctant assessment is `CAUTIOUS_RESEARCH_ONLY`. Incomplete records yield `NOT_READY` / `WITHHELD`. Missing dossiers have no completion percentage. The dashboard dates recorded coverage and withholds a current ready grade once evidence is stale or another scientific gate fails. It displays original-source links, critical evaluation and uncertainty in the trial dossier.

The composer additionally requires the dossier's declared FDA application to match the reviewed OUTCOME application, its indication to match the historical target, and its company CIK to match the reviewed MAIN company. These string/identity checks reject mismatches; independent application/trial/drug/indication/issuer verification still requires the underlying original documents and review. They do not establish external source authenticity.

The authenticated reviewed-manifest endpoint is opt-in and read-only. No real dossier or model-eligible historical cohort is installed by these changes. Synthetic test fixtures are isolated under frontend tests and are not loaded as product data. All composed outputs retain `modelValidated: false`, `probability: null`, and `tradeAuthorized: false`. No real or paper trading is authorized.

The trial panel separately shows SCIENCE review counts, OUTCOME readiness, MAIN readiness and the number of ready prerequisite groups out of the five declared DECISION groups. This final count is not a project completion percentage. The existing dated MAIN platform acceptance inventory remains a different denominator. No estimated completion date is generated without comparable verified observations.
