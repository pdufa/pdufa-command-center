## New material pre-cutoff signal — manufacturing

The **2026-03-24 10-K** (fiscal year 2025) explicitly disclosed that a third-party manufacturer named in the NDA had undergone an FDA cGMP inspection, with **two observations relating to solid oral dosage manufacturing**. The sponsor reported an ongoing remedial-action dialogue and cautioned that approval could be delayed beyond June 20, 2026. This source was publicly available before the 2026-05-21 cutoff: https://ir.achieverx.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001193125-26-120690/achv-20251231.htm

A **2026-04-16 exhibit** also publicly restated the June 20 PDUFA target and the two pivotal phase 3 studies: https://ir.achievelifesciences.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001193125-26-159189/achv-ex99_1.htm

**Interpretation permitted before cutoff:** manufacturing compliance represented a **documented material regulatory risk**, distinct from the favorable placebo-controlled efficacy findings of ORCA-2 and ORCA-3. The pre-cutoff 10-K did **not** establish the ultimate FDA decision or its final rationale. Do not convert a disclosed risk into a certain rejection without a prospectively frozen decision rule.

## 2026-11-19 CORRECTION: stronger pre-cutoff disclosure on May 12, 2026

**Critical chronology update.** The March 24, 2026 10-K identified *possible* delay from two manufacturing cGMP observations. But a later document, **Achieve's May 12, 2026 Form 10-Q for quarter ended March 31, 2026**, made a substantially stronger statement **before the 2026-05-21 research cutoff**:

> We expect to receive a Complete Response Letter, or CRL, from the FDA on or before our June 20, 2026 PDUFA targeted action date, which would delay our NDA approval.

**Primary source:** https://ir.achieverx.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001193125-26-218177/achv-20260331.htm (dated May 12, 2026). The filing also reported that the manufacturing facility had received an **Official Action Indicated (OAI)** classification and warning letter, with two observations relating to solid oral dosage manufacturing. These were general cGMP issues at the third-party facility, not necessarily specific to cytisinicline.

**Correct interpretation at cutoff:** As of 2026-05-21, a reviewer using permitted public information would have known not merely that manufacturing issues *might* delay approval, but that **the sponsor itself publicly expected a CRL**. This is a material **Level 6 regulatory-risk signal** that must be retained in any correctly dated pre-decision evidence packet. It is not a prediction generated by this investigator and should not be scored as one.

**Independent outcome-only cross-check (quarantined from blind packet):** The original FDA redacted June 20, 2026 CRL, https://download.open.fda.gov/crl/CRL_NDA218995_20260620_Redacted.pdf, confirms the historical outcome was **CRL**, citing facility inspection deficiencies and outstanding prescribing information. It is **post-cutoff evidence** and must never be shown to a purported blind assessor before a genuine lock. The post-decision sponsor statement https://ir.achieverx.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/264/achieve-life-sciences-receives-complete-response-letter-from-fda-for-cytisinicline-nda likewise belongs to outcome-only records.

**Methodological consequence:** A model that retrospectively calls CRL after seeing this record has **no blind-accuracy credit** unless a demonstrably independent, outcome-unexposed assessor had already locked that prediction. If the backtest cutoff is 30 days before PDUFA, the May 12 sponsor expectation is eligible; if an alternative earlier cutoff is adopted, it must be declared prospectively and applied consistently to all cases. Do not silently omit the May 12 disclosure to simulate uncertainty.
