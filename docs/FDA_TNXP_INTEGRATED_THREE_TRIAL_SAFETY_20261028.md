### Derived descriptive comparisons

- RALLY treatment discontinuation exceeds RELIEF by 6.7 percentage
  points and RESILIENT by 9.1 percentage points.
- RALLY placebo discontinuation exceeds RELIEF and RESILIENT by 2.7
  percentage points. This suggests broader trial-conduct or
  population differences may have contributed; it does **not**
  establish that COVID, missingness or tolerability caused failure.
- RESILIENT oral-site adverse-event absolute excess: 32.7 percentage
  points. Local effects were commonly transient per sponsor reports.
- RESILIENT treatment-versus-placebo AE-discontinuation difference:
  2.6 percentage points. Study discontinuation is not equivalent to
  a drug-attributable adverse reaction.

### Unresolved science gates

Need integrated safety summary, protocol/CSR datasets, adverse-event
coding and severity by arm, exposure-adjusted incidence, dropout
timing, causality adjudication, special populations, drug interactions,
and sensitivity analyses for missing efficacy outcomes. Do not
compare cross-trial percentages causally without harmonizing
definitions and ascertainment.
