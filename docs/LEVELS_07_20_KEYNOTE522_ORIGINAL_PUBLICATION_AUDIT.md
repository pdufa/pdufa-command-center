## Verified publication findings to extract
- Randomized 1174 patients: pembrolizumab + chemotherapy n=784, placebo + chemotherapy n=390.
- Pathological complete response primary interim cohort n=602: 260/401 (64.8%) versus 103/201 (51.2%), difference 13.6 percentage points (95% CI 5.4–21.8), P<0.001. pCR data cutoff 2018-09-24.
- Early EFS second interim analysis cutoff 2019-04-24: median follow-up 15.5 months; 58/784 versus 46/390 events; HR 0.63 (95% CI 0.43–0.93). This is not mature final EFS.
- 18-month event-free estimates 91.3% versus 85.3%, from 104 observed events against 327 expected at final analysis.
- Treatment-related grade >=3 adverse events across phases: 78.0% versus 73.0%; neoadjuvant phase serious treatment-related adverse events: 32.5% versus 19.5%; neoadjuvant phase discontinuation of any trial drug due to treatment-related adverse events: 23.3% versus 12.3%.
- Trial was double-blind; PD-L1 status not an enrollment restriction; primary endpoints pCR and EFS. Stratified by nodal status, tumor size and carboplatin schedule.
- Note safety denominators (treated: 781 versus 389) differ from randomized denominators.
