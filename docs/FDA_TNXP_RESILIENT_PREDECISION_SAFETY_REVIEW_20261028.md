2025-08-15. Source is 2024 SEC-filed sponsor presentation:

Sponsor-reported serious adverse events in the treatment arm
included renal carcinoma deemed unrelated to drug and pancreatitis
starting 14 days after treatment completion, judged *possibly related*.
Timing of the participant's final dose was uncertain. This is a
signal for adjudication, not proof of drug causation.

## Interpretation

- Absolute excess oral-site AE rate: 32.7 percentage points.
- Absolute excess AE-related discontinuation: 2.6 percentage points.
- Low counts of serious adverse events cannot exclude uncommon risks.
- Oral tolerability and dosing-site reactions may affect adherence.
- Compare exposure, discontinuation and events in RELIEF and RALLY
  before extrapolating cross-trial safety consistency.
- The sponsor's statement that no new safety signals were observed
  is a characterization, not independent safety certification.
