# Source-validation checkpoint: nine unlisted PubMed hits are not direct trial efficacy evidence

PubMed exact-NCT text search and trial registration references are discovery, not
an adjudicated clinical efficacy result. Nine trial–PMID associations across
four FOCUS NCTs were missing from the original registry reference list.
Inspection of PubMed titles, abstracts and the original study topic found
background, trial design, outcomes methodology and a different drug trial.
