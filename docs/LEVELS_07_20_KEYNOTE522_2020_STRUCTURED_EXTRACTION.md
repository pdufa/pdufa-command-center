## Results published in 2020
- Phase 3 randomized allocation: 2:1; pembrolizumab arm 784, placebo arm 390.
- Population: previously untreated stage II or III triple-negative breast cancer.
- Pathological complete response interim analysis: first 602 randomized participants.
- Pathological complete response: pembrolizumab + chemotherapy 64.8% (95% CI 59.9–69.5); placebo + chemotherapy 51.2% (95% CI 44.1–58.3).
- Estimated absolute difference: 13.6 percentage points (95% CI 5.4–21.8); p < 0.001.
- Median follow-up: 15.5 months.
- Events reported at that follow-up: 58/784 (7.4%) vs 46/390 (11.8%), hazard ratio 0.63 (95% CI 0.43–0.93). These are early event counts, **not** a mature final EFS analysis.
- Grade 3 or higher treatment-related adverse events: 78.0% vs 73.0%; treatment-related deaths 3 vs 1.
