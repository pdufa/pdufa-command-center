## PDUFA26-TIMING-038 — RP1 (vusolimogene oderparepvec) plus nivolumab, anti-PD-1-resistant melanoma

Ledger: BLA125827, original STN-0/Class 1 resubmission, provisional research cutoff 2026-07-07; IGNYTE NCT03767348. Primary paper: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40627813/ ; PubMed independently confirms **Epub 2025-07-08**, predating the provisional cutoff. It also confirms trial registration, reported response and safety results.

| Level | Scientific finding | Remaining requirement |
|---|---|---|
| L01 | Drug, combination, indication and NCT03767348 match the primary publication. | Verify the exact resubmission application number, cycle and PDUFA target date. |
| L02 | Open-label, single-arm IGNYTE cohort of 140 adults with confirmed progression after anti-PD-1; independently centrally reviewed RECIST 1.1 objective response. | Protocol, selection bias, statistical plan and rationale for lack of randomized control. |
| L03 | Independently confirmed PubMed abstract: ORR 32.9% (95% CI 25.2–41.3%), 15.0% complete response, median duration of response 33.7 months (95% CI 14.1–not reached). **These are noncomparative estimates**, not evidence of superiority over alternatives. | Full analysis population, missingness, subgroup durability and how resubmission addresses prior regulatory deficiencies. |
| L04 | PubMed reports treatment-related adverse events 77.1% grade 1/2, 9.3% grade 3, 3.6% grade 4 and no grade 5. | Immune-mediated harms, oncolytic-virus risks, serious events and exposure follow-up. |
| L05 | Population had advanced melanoma with anti-PD-1 treatment failure; cannot generalize to untreated melanoma. | Exact requested label, prior-treatment criteria and subgroup relevance. |
| L06 | The case is a provisional Class 1 resubmission. Clinical publication cannot establish correction of any original deficiencies. | Confirm actual FDA review cycle, complete response remediation, CMC, inspection and label readiness. |

**Disposition:** Primary publication abstract and Epub date verified; review-cycle identity and full pivotal evidence remain incomplete. No APPROVE/CRL lock.
