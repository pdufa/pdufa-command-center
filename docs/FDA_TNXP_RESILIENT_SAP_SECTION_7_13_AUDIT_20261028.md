# RESILIENT F307 — SAP Sections 7 and 13 audit (research only)

Reviewed 2026-10-28; historical FDA action cutoff 2025-08-15.

## Documentary evidence

Statistical Analysis Plan Final v1.0, dated 17 October 2023:
https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/49/NCT05273749/SAP_001.pdf

- **Section 7, printed page 16 (PDF page 16):** ITT is defined as
  **all randomized patients** and is the primary efficacy population,
  analyzed by randomized assignment. Safety population comprises
  patients who took investigational product, analyzed as treated.
- **Section 13, printed page 37 (PDF page 37):** changes from planned
  analysis list an addition concerning PGIC as a continuous variable.
  No ITT exclusion for a participant randomized in error is listed.
  This is the October 2023 SAP, **not** proof that a subsequent
  final SAP amendment or CSR explanation does not exist.
- **Section 4.2, printed pages 14–15:** describes randomization
  eligibility checks; a mistaken randomization can violate screening
  criteria, but this text alone does not authorize postrandomization
  removal from the ITT efficacy population.

ACR 2024 F307 poster:
https://www.tonixpharma.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/ACR-2024-Poster-F307-Trial_vFinal2_10-15-2024_QRcode.pdf

- The sponsor reports that one placebo subject randomized in error
  due to a safety criterion was included in safety but excluded
  from efficacy ITT: 457 randomized/safety vs 456 efficacy ITT.

## Adjudication

**Numerical difference: EXPLAINED (sponsor report).**
**Prespecified ITT conformance: OPEN / POTENTIAL CONFLICT.**

The October 2023 SAP definition and sponsor-reported exclusion are
not facially consistent. It would be incorrect to call the exclusion
definitively improper without verifying whether a superseding
analysis plan, prespecified deviation rule, FDA agreement, or CSR
amendment existed. Do not infer that including the subject would
alter or preserve statistical significance without participant-level
data and reanalysis.
