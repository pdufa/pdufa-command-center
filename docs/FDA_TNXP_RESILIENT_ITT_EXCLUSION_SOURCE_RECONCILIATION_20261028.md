# TNXP RESILIENT: excluded placebo participant — source reconciliation

Research-only, prepared 2026-10-28. FDA decision cutoff 2025-08-15.

   https://www.tonixpharma.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/ACR-2024-Poster-F307-Trial_vFinal2_10-15-2024_QRcode.pdf

## Finding and remaining concern

The **numerical discrepancy is explained by the sponsor's 2024
poster**: one randomized placebo participant was excluded from the
reported efficacy ITT set after being randomized in error. But the
protocol and SAP use an all-randomized ITT definition. Therefore,
the **consistency of the exclusion with the prespecified analysis
plan is not established**. Do not mark this discrepancy fully
resolved until original CSR, final SAP amendments, deviation log,
and sensitivity analysis including all 457 are reviewed.

The clinicaltrials.gov-hosted protocol/SAP documents' upload dates
and exact public availability before 2025-08-15 must be checked
separately. Their 2023 internal dates alone do not prove public
availability by the historical decision cutoff. The ACR poster
was available by late 2024.

**Scientific evidence gate:** population difference explained;
protocol-conformance check still OPEN. Source bytes not retained
or hashed; no independent CSR validation. No historical forecast,
calibrated PoA or trading recommendation.
