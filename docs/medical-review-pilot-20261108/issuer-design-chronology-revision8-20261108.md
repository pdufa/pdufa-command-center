### Official historical source retrieval outcome

An isolated, credential-free research workflow tried the historical internal endpoints documented by the independent open-source ctrdata R package: `/api/int/studies/NCT06081894?history=true`, and original version API indices 25, 26, 28 and 29. The response was **HTTP 403 for each request**, so the workflow **did not retrieve original full historical registry versions**. The [GitHub Actions run and error receipt](https://github.com/pdufa/pdufa-command-center/actions/runs/42527582798) preserve the limitation. **Never claim official versions 26/27 have been downloaded, or that the historical endpoint audit succeeded.** The official browser history and the issuer's contemporaneous original poster nevertheless support a dated design-history account.

### Scientific adjudication implications (no grades)

- **4.2 Endpoint hierarchy:** Former single-primary design is substantiated by the original public 2023 study poster. Later dual-primary design is substantiated by the original retrieved 2025 protocol. **Redesign occurred** across source periods. Its earliest approved amendment and SAP lock timing remain unverified.
- **4.3 Estimands/missingness:** The dated Amendment 04 defines key estimands, but the **final signed SAP, prespecified missing-data model and sensitivity analyses remain unavailable**.
- **4.4 Sample-size/power:** Sponsor documented intended increase from 420 to ≥500, with 90% power at alpha 0.025 each, and public results report 517 treated/randomized. The diagram value `N=420` retained in some newer decks may be a stale graphic; do not treat it as an actual enrollment denominator.
- **5.1/5.2 Primary inference and alpha control:** Positive source-reported p-values exist. A full multiplicity/alpha-recycling integrity audit requires the final SAP and dated original graph; do not equate a redesign to an uncorrected multiple test or clinical failure.
- **8.2 Red-team:** Obtain signed EU/Japanese regulator correspondence only if lawful/public; sponsor's description of ex-US regulator feedback is not independent evidence of the regulators' position. Check database-lock/unblinding and amendment dates before assigning any adverse compliance finding.
