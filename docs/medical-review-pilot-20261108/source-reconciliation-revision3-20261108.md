## NEW methodological evidence from accessible full-text secondary analysis

1. **Study population and allocation description (4.1, 4.4):** The responder paper describes 517 randomized participants at approximately 160 sites, a 36-week primary follow-up, allocation by an Interactive Web Response System, allocation concealment, trial masking, and randomization strata involving persistent atrial fibrillation and intracavitary obstruction. This is an author report, not original trial-log verification.
2. **Secondary-analysis prespecification (4.2, 4.3, 5.2):** Authors state that multidomain responder work was added to the trial SAP before database lock and unblinding. This is **not documentary proof** of the date/version; require locked SAP and amendments. Distinguish that analysis from the pivotal co-primary endpoint testing and from other post hoc echocardiography analyses.
3. **Missingness and statistical threshold (4.3, 5.2):** Secondary responder analysis used the last observation available from weeks 8–36 when week-36 measures were absent and classified those without post-week-8 data as nonresponders. It explicitly reports **no multiple-comparison adjustment** for responder comparisons. These are secondary methods; do not silently infer the same approach for pivotal co-primary estimands.
4. **Responder efficacy (5.1, 5.2, 5.4):** At week 36, the secondary report describes ≥3 favorable outcome measures in **136/258 (53%) vs 36/259 (13%)**. It reports ≥0.5 mL/kg/min pVO2 improvement in **115 (45%) vs 97 (37%), P=0.1** on a binary responder comparison. The pivotal continuous pVO2 estimate remained statistically significant (mean difference 0.67 mL/kg/min, P=0.003). These are different estimands/methods, not automatically contradictory, and the secondary p-values are unadjusted for multiplicity.
5. **Safety reconciliation issue (5.3, 8.2):** The full-text responder paper lists **serious heart-failure adverse events 11 (4.3%) aficamten vs 1 (0.4%) placebo**. Sponsor's later reports give **12 (4.7%) vs 3 (1.2%)**. **Classify as OPEN SOURCE DISCORDANCE — not confirmed reporting error or an adjudicated event difference.** Require exact event-definition, dataset, safety cutoff, denominator and SAE-coding reconciliation. **Keep distinct** from all-cause serious adverse events 52/258 and 38/259.
6. **Apparent arithmetic inconsistency in indexed secondary safety narrative (5.3, 8.2):** The PMC text describes treatment interruption after LVEF decline as `14 (2.7%)` aficamten and `1 (0.8%)` placebo, although the trial's cited randomized groups are 258/259. **Numerators, denominators and printed percentages do not align on simple whole-arm arithmetic.** Could involve different analysis populations, text extraction, a table/transcription issue or an erratum; do **not** choose a corrected rate or accuse authors without checking the original publication, correction notices and safety population table. Record as `REQUIRES_ORIGINAL_TABLE_RECONCILIATION`.
7. **Design-specific caution (4.4, 5.4):** Randomization strata named in Methods (atrial fibrillation, intracavitary obstruction) are not the same as the adjustment factors described for secondary outcome regressions (exercise-test modality, beta-blocker use). This is not inherently a contradiction; ask whether the final SAP anticipated those covariates.
8. **External validity and durability (5.4, 5.5):** Authors acknowledge categorical threshold sensitivity, short follow-up for long-term outcomes and unvalidated within-patient clinical-meaning thresholds for several surrogate measures. Five endpoint domains derived from **one trial** do not create five independent pivotal trials.
