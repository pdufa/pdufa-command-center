# MAIN Stage 4 — ACACIA provisional scientific evidence and hypothetical FDA pathways

As-of source snapshot: 2026-11-08. Exact pilot: NCT06081894 / CY 6033 / ACACIA-HCM, aficamten for symptomatic nonobstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy.

## Provisional evidence interpretation

The original [Phase III primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42663312/) reported favorable week-36 co-primary effects: KCCQ-CSS +3.0 points (95% CI 0.5 to 5.5) and peak oxygen uptake +0.67 mL/kg/min (95% CI 0.22 to 1.11). These establish reported statistical direction, not independent assessment of clinical importance or approval odds.

The published left-ventricular ejection fraction less than 50% signal was 27/258 versus 2/259; all serious adverse events were 52/258 versus 38/259. Unadjusted risk differences of 9.69 and 5.48 percentage points are descriptive, not adjusted causal risk. Separate heart-failure serious-AE reports conflict (11/1 versus 12/3) and are not merged. The final locked CY 6033 SAP, case definitions and underlying analysis tables have not been independently reconciled.

The [issuer November 4 2026 SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1061983/000119312526447408/cytk-20260930.htm) said the nHCM supplemental NDA remained planned for Q4. This snapshot does not establish an accepted nHCM application or its own PDUFA date; an obstructive-HCM application cannot be used as its substitute.

## What this development change does

- A deterministic source-and-identity-checked interpretation of six research domains for the exact pilot, with original source links and qualitative HIGH UNQUANTIFIED uncertainty.
- A user-controlled hypothetical scenario rule with four pathway names: PRE_FILING, CONDITIONAL_APPROVAL_PATH, REGULATORY_CONCERN_PATH and ADDITIONAL_REVIEW_PATH. These are scenarios to organize questions and do not represent FDA probability categories.
- The site always retains approval, CRL and delay as ABSTAIN, accuracy as NOT CALIBRATED, and 0 independent method grades. Changing assumptions has no persistence or API writes.
- Fails closed for wrong NCT, changed safety denominators, source URLs, Phase III effect values, 18-mapped-position roster, true medical grade events, or a claimed accepted filing without updating the source contract.

This is not a trained empirical model, nor a scientific signed grade or physician determination. It makes visible the preliminary evidence direction and conditional pathways without inventing percentages or claiming prediction accuracy.

## Gates to actual FDA prediction

1. Independently reconcile source protocol and final locked SAP, statistical missingness, multiplicity and benefit-risk.
2. Verify exact trial phase/indication and obtain authorized human scientific method review; preserve append-only history.
3. Build a complete, time-stamped, independently adjudicated historical application-level outcome cohort, including CRL and documented review delays, plus application/program holdouts.
4. Fit and freeze the separate target-specific forecast models, then assess held-out Brier/log loss, calibration error and uncertainty intervals prior to releasing non-ABSTAIN probabilities.
5. Verify signed-in isolated preview, regression, and independent release review. Do not merge the main Stage 4 draft or change production because of passing source-rule tests.
