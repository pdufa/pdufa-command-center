# Retained Level 1–6 research package versus newer FOCUS roster

The source-bound Level 1–6 package is frozen at November 14, 2026.
The current FOCUS roster has advanced to November 16. A later roster date
does not mean the old research packet was recollected on that date.

The exporter uses the current roster to check the same 32 exact NCTs.
Its historical rosterAsOf field is bounded by the original research cutoff.
Original observations, clinical scores and prediction history are untouched.
Any newly retrieved clinical data must go into a new versioned package.

This preserves dated research evidence without claiming freshness or
whole-level scientific completion. CI must pass before engineering validation.
