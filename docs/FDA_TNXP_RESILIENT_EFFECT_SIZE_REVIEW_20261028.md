# TNXP RESILIENT: predecision clinical magnitude and uncertainty

Research-only analytical supplement, 2026-10-28.
Source: SEC-filed sponsor presentation dated 2024-06-10,
https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1430306/000183988224018368/ex99-02.htm
This source predates FDA's 2025-08-15 action; sponsor-provided data
are not independently authenticated against the protocol or CSR.

## Prespecified and descriptive endpoints

- RESILIENT primary 0–10 numerical rating scale, week 14:
  LS mean change active -1.82 (SE 0.12), placebo -1.16 (SE 0.12).
  Between-arm difference -0.65 (SE 0.16); p=0.00005.
- >=30% pain reduction: 45.9% active, 27.1% placebo;
  absolute risk difference 18.8 percentage points,
  approximate NNT 5.32 (descriptive; not adjusted for missingness).
- >=50% pain reduction: 22.5% active, 13.3% placebo;
  absolute risk difference 9.2 percentage points,
  approximate NNT 10.87 (descriptive; not adjusted for missingness).
- Patient Global Impression of Change responders:
  35.1% active, 19.1% placebo; difference 16 percentage points.
- Sponsor reports serial gatekeeping for key secondary endpoints,
  but no independent SAP/protocol review has been performed.
- Sponsor presentation identifies two positive Phase 3 trials
  (RELIEF, RESILIENT) and one failed primary endpoint (RALLY).

## Interpretation and limitations

The continuous primary endpoint is statistically convincing in
RESILIENT, but a 0.65-point mean advantage is modest in absolute
terms. Responder differences may be more clinically interpretable,
though the published presentation is insufficient to audit
imputation, estimand choice, attrition, and protocol deviations.
Approximate NNT values use rounded displayed percentages and should
not be treated as prespecified, independently validated outcomes.
The failed RALLY trial must be analyzed separately, including
whether any explanation was prespecified and independently supported.

These calculations are a retrospective scientific *description*, not
a forecast. No calibrated probability, blinded validation, or
independently verified 50-level dossier is claimed. FDA outcome-only
sources must not enter the predecision feature set.
