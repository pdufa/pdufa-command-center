## Preliminary interpretation

The primary efficacy replication signal is MIXED: two successful
studies and one failed study. RALLY's alternative analysis without
imputation is statistically significant but cannot convert the
failed primary result into success. Higher RALLY discontinuation
rates may help explain imputation sensitivity; causality remains
unproven. RESILIENT reports lower AE discontinuation rates and
stronger primary efficacy than RALLY, but cross-trial comparisons
are not randomized and cannot establish why effects differ.
