# HOP historical comparisons: point-in-time safety

**Status:** Research-only. No FDA approval, complete-response-letter, or delay probability is authorized.

Historical cases must be independently adjudicated **before** their outcomes may
appear in a descriptive comparator list. A CSV that merely passes schema validation,
a source URL, a successful FDA PDF download, or an existing archived clinical-trial
record is not independent adjudication.

## Three different dates

1. `public_features_as_of`: the date the clinical features used for matching
   were actually publicly available. It must be strictly **before** the
   historical FDA decision, not filled in after knowing the result.
2. `decision_date`: the historical FDA decision effective date.
   It must precede the prediction's cutoff.
3. `evidence_as_of`: the earliest verified public date of FDA evidence
   supporting that historical outcome. It must be **on or after**
   `decision_date`, and strictly before the target's `prediction_as_of`.
   With date-only precision, same-day label publication is excluded.

For example, a **fictional** decision on 2020-01-10 with clinical evidence
public on 2019-12-01 and FDA decision evidence public on 2020-01-11 may be
considered for a 2020-02-01 prediction, after independent adjudication.
It must not be considered for a prediction dated 2020-01-10.

## Comparator gates

- Official HTTPS FDA decision source with no credential, port or fragment spoofing.
- Independently adjudicated **outcome**, explicitly documented before inclusion.
- Proven predecision availability of all matching clinical features.
- Recognized binary FDA action and phase 3–4 case.
- Exact canonical application and positive review-cycle identity; no duplicate
  case IDs or application/cycle claims. All repeated cycle claims are excluded
  before filtering source quality or adjudication flags, in either input order.
- No outcome evidence first available on/after the target prediction date.
- No probability, predicted outcome, trading signal, or model readiness
  generated by the comparator. A similarity score is not a likelihood.

The manual `hop_outcomes_import.py` intake returns only quarantined data.
It has no path that sets `independently_adjudicated=True`.
The comparison helper's Python input flags must reflect a separate,
documented independent review and cannot themselves be used as proof
of that review.

**Remaining release gates:** verified review-cycle/application identity,
complete filing/decision denominator, duplicate-cycle reconciliation,
predecision feature provenance and clinical source review, selection-bias
audit, time-ordered out-of-sample accuracy/calibration including explicit
abstention, and a separately approved deployment. Delay is not a
synonym for CRL or a final approval state.
