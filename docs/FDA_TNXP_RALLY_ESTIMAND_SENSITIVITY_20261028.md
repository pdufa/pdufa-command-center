# TNXP RALLY: prespecified primary versus sensitivity analysis

Research-only historical predecision evidence review (2026-10-28).
Source: SEC-filed March 21, 2022 announcement:
https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1430306/000138713122003807/ex99-01.htm

| Analysis | RALLY pain LS mean difference (SE) | p value | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Prespecified primary MMRM + multiple imputation | -0.2 (0.16) | 0.115 | Failed |
| Prespecified MMRM without multiple imputation | -0.4 (0.16) | 0.014 | Sensitivity, not a substitute for failed primary |
| RELIEF primary MMRM + multiple imputation | -0.4 (0.16) | 0.010 | Positive comparison |

Adverse-event-related discontinuation: RALLY active 15.2%, placebo
6.2%; RELIEF active 8.5%, placebo 3.5%. The sponsor attributes
attenuation partly to imputation and higher discontinuation, but the
causal explanation is **not independently established**.

**Assessment rule:** Never promote a secondary or sensitivity p value
to the status of a successful primary endpoint. The original RALLY
failure remains a negative replication signal in any retrospective
TNXP science review.

Still needed: protocol, full SAP, missing-not-at-random tipping-point
analysis, clinical study reports, reasons and timing of dropout,
prespecified estimands, and independent source review.

Source was published before the 2025-08-15 decision, but original
bytes are not retained/hashed. No outcome leakage, calibrated PoA,
valid prospective backtest, or trading authorization is claimed.
