## Scientific worksheets and limits

The companion [JSON worksheets](focus-source-research-20261113.json) contain six levels for every trial: questions, phase-appropriate requirements, traceable preliminary findings/reasons, contradiction-review status, limitations, source/reviewer requirements and abstention conditions. Clinical efficacy, significance and endpoint medical importance are not adjudicated.

The two public PDFs for NCT05507203 have title-page observations for protocol v5.1 (April 10, 2024) and SAP v2.0 (July 15, 2025). The SAP chronology must be reconciled with primary-completion, database-lock and unblinding records; dates alone do not demonstrate post-hoc analysis or a deficiency. No PDF body or confidential record is committed.

## Reproduce and review

The acquisition module requires an evidence root outside Git, an explicit cutoff and a new output snapshot. It refuses changed original bytes and overwriting research snapshots. Acquisition is manual and bounded, never a new scan schedule. `readiness_report(...)` builds the existing phase-first report; the existing renderer accepts `--preliminary-research` for an escaped offline preview. No website is published.

FDA portal, issuer and publication links are discovery requirements, not authenticated regulatory/application evidence. Full protocols/SAPs/results/publications, source ownership, document history, exact application identity and all medical/statistical/specialist/challenge reviews remain outstanding.

The [owner approval package](../../docs/level16-owner-approval-package.md) and [operations/rollback package](../../docs/level16-staging-operations.md) describe external decisions. No unreviewed worksheet can authorize an assessment or feed historical prediction/calibration.
