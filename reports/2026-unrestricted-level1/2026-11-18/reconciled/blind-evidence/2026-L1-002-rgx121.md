## Pre-cutoff contemporaneous source (SEC filing August 18, 2025)

- REGENXBIO disclosed an FDA extension of the PDUFA goal **from November 9, 2025 to February 8, 2026**, to review additional clinical data.
- The sponsor submitted longer-term **12-month follow-up for 13 pivotal-study patients** in response to an FDA information request. The press release characterizes the data as positive and consistent with previously submitted biomarker and neurodevelopmental findings, but **does not give a quantitative efficacy effect or statistical test**.
- The company reported that the August 2025 FDA pre-license and bioresearch monitoring inspections had **no observations** and that the FDA had raised **no safety-related concerns at that point**. These are sponsor-reported, time-limited statements, not proof of approvability.
- The proposed biological mechanism is CNS delivery of an iduronate-2-sulfatase gene via an AAV vector. The sponsor described HS D2S6 as a relevant biomarker; **surrogate validation for clinical benefit must be independently assessed**.
- Regulatory designations disclosed: orphan, rare pediatric disease, fast track, and RMAT. These designations do not establish approval.

## Additional primary contemporaneous efficacy evidence (located November 18, 2026; published before historical cutoff)

- **February 7, 2024, REGENXBIO pivotal topline release:** the pivotal CAMPSIITE phase reportedly met its prespecified week-16 CSF D2S6 biomarker endpoint, **p=0.00016**, with an **86% median reduction**. The sponsor also reported 25 participants dosed across all trial phases as of January 3, 2024. This is an earlier analysis cut, not the later one-year pivotal dataset. Source: https://ir.regenxbio.com/news-releases/news-release-details/regenxbio-announces-pivotal-trial-rgx-121-treatment-mps-ii
- **September 5, 2025, REGENXBIO one-year pivotal release:** among **13 pivotal participants**, the sponsor reported **82% median reduction** in CSF HS D2S6 sustained through one year, week-16 endpoint **p<0.0001**, and neurodevelopmental skill acquisition or stability on BSID-III subscales at one year. This is sponsor-reported evidence and does not independently establish surrogacy, causal neurodevelopmental benefit, or a valid natural-history comparator. Source: https://ir.regenxbio.com/news-releases/news-release-details/regenxbio-presents-positive-twelve-month-pivotal-data-phase
- **September 2025 sponsor congress slide deck:** week 16 **9/13** below the attenuated threshold, week 24 **10/13**, one year **8/11** with two one-year samples unobtainable; corresponding median reductions **81%, 82%, 82%**. Slide deck states week-16 **p<0.0001** compared against a 20% margin. This 2025 endpoint definition and threshold need independent protocol/SAP corroboration.
