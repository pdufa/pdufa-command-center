## Additional contemporaneous clinical evidence (source-published before cutoff)

- **January 31, 2024 — Atara/Pierre Fabre ALLELE Phase 3 publication announcement.** ALLELE reported **22/43 objective responses (51.2%)** in relapsed/refractory EBV+ PTLD; **median response duration 23.0 months**, **median overall survival 18.4 months**. An October 2023 data-cut analysis was reported as **49% ORR, p<0.0001**; these are different analysis cuts/populations and must not be pooled or conflated. Source: https://www.pierrefabrepharmaceuticals.com/sites/default/files/press/20240131_PR_Allele_Ebvallo_Lancet.pdf
- **2025 ALLELE updated abstract, ICML/Hematological Oncology.** Overall **38/75 ORR (50.7%; 95% CI 38.9–62.4%)**, with **21/75 complete responses (28.0%)**, **median DOR 23.0 months**. Serious treatment-emergent adverse events occurred in **65.4% of HCT** and **61.2% of SOT** recipients; fatal treatment-emergent adverse events in **19.2% HCT** and **18.4% SOT**, none attributed to treatment in the abstract. These are treatment-emergent events in a seriously ill population, not proven drug-caused events.
