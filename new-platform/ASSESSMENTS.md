# Candidate assessments v1

The exact Pipeline universe is evaluated by three independent 100-point research models: Pipeline P1–P7 (25/20/15/15/10/10/5), Gem Jr J1–J7 (20/15/20/15/15/10/5), and Gem G1–G7 (25/20/15/15/10/10/5). These are transparent, uncalibrated research heuristics, not probabilities or trade signals. Screening and saved membership are independent; second financing remains an optional filter.

`app/candidate_assessments.py` is the scoring authority. The score index covers every immutable Pipeline ID, including missing or unlinked records. It never merges trials by ticker. Registry design measurements award explicit rule-based points. Endpoint success requires exact-trial adjudication. A registry results posting, smallest p-value, study completion, market cap, or absence of a safety flag cannot substitute for clinical, safety, statistical, commercial or valuation analysis.

Missing, stale, conflicting and future-dated evidence is unscored. Observed points and assessed weights are both displayed; incomplete totals are never normalized to 100 or ranked as complete. All seven categories are present, including categories with no evidence. Detailed records preserve all reported endpoints, comparison groups, p-value qualifiers, estimates and confidence intervals, with links to the complete saved registry sheet.

Analyst-reviewed measurements reside in `data/candidate_assessment_reviews.json`, keyed by exact Pipeline identity. Each metric requires a grade 0–4, actual `measurements`, `rationale`, HTTPS `source`, `sourceDate` and `reviewedAt`. Grade 0 means adverse/unsupported evidence; 1 weak; 2 mixed; 3 supportive; 4 strong. `points = maximum × grade / 4`. The UI exposes this rule and the actual grade. Dates and review periods are enforced. An optional `conflict: true` withholds points. Empty reviews do not assert analysis was done. Metrics and review windows are defined in the model, so a rule change must increment the model version.

`app/assessment_service.py` builds a compact full-universe index from the saved archive and published source snapshot. A dedicated append-only database table preserves evidence fingerprints and compact score/coverage changes; unchanged evidence produces no duplicate history. Migration read-only mode suppresses history writes. Existing checklists, permanent IDs and GEM stage history are not rewritten. The browser refreshes the index every five minutes while open; a new source version, registry revision or Pacific day invalidates the backend cache. Startup warms the same index without starting an additional scanner or schedule.

Authenticated endpoints: `/api/candidate-assessments` (index) and `/api/candidate-assessments/detail?identity=...` (exact-program detail). The Next.js authenticated proxy is `/api/assessments`. Public health exposes aggregate completion counts only. Initial index construction returns `BUILDING`; details wait for a complete index and reject a changed evidence snapshot. All data remains private behind the existing dashboard authentication.
