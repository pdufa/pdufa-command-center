# Read-only 800-point science source-dependency map

The exact-NCT trial page's **800-point evidence dependency map** links the
pre-existing 16 evidence-input requirements to the 8 scientific assessment
layers. Each layer has 5 assessments × 20 methodological depths = 100
**planned pending** reviews. The full plan still has 800 tasks.

This is NOT 800 actual assessments, a submission of evidence, reviewer
authentication, an independently verified clinical finding, or an FDA
outcome forecast.

## Audit logic

- Re-validates the complete old 8-depth 320-task review inventory (all pending)
  and the exact trial identity via the existing protected validator.
- Separately re-validates all 16 archived and external evidence requirements,
  including absence of independent review for the four non-registry gates.
- Cross-checks that the two sources refer to the same exact NCT and that the
  per-layer crosswalk covers every one of the 16 requirement identifiers.
- Labels archived source *presence* separately from verified scientific
  truth. A checksum confirms saved bytes were unchanged, not that the
  study was independently analyzed or a regulatory outcome forecasted.
- Always reports 0 completed tasks, 0 verified sources, no scientific
  conclusion or FDA approval probability. Invalid or inconsistent data
  hides the new mapping rather than displaying a partial success.
- Shows a collapsible layer summary with which requirement is already
  present in the archive, and a concrete next action for independent review.
  It leaves the original 20-stage archive source inventory, 320-task A/B
  workflow, 800-row CSV and existing source PDF-byte checks unchanged.

## Decision gates before any actual science

Obtain dated original protocol/SAP versions; reconcile posted efficacy and
safety results with the full publications and clinical reports; inspect
independent biological corroboration and FDA/CMC documentation. Identify
and verify two qualified independent human reviewers, capture signoffs,
resolve disagreements, and validate each exact-NCT claim. These processes
must not be simulated by rows or generated grades.

Do not promote the planning map to a clinical score, approval probability,
GEM/WT qualification result, automatic scan/trade action, or production
deployment. Only the isolated science integration preview may use this
review UI until separate production approval is granted.
