### Evidence in favor of approval

1. Global randomized, double-blind phase 3 study with relevant patient population and a statistically significant progression-free survival benefit. Sponsor's 2025 scientific presentation reported IRRC PFS HR **0.52**, 95% CI **0.41–0.66**, p<0.0001, median **6.8 vs 4.4 months**.
2. Global overall survival results improved with follow-up: June 2026 cut HR **0.76**, 95% CI **0.61–0.95**, *nominal* p=0.0151. Western subgroup HR **0.76**, 95% CI **0.52–1.10**. The positive trend supports potential clinical benefit but is not an independently established prespecified OS success.
3. Unmet need after EGFR-targeted therapy and supportive findings in the separate China HARMONi-A program. These results are context, not independent replication of every HARMONi participant and not a replacement for the U.S. global application.
4. Sponsor says safety remains manageable and no new adverse signal was observed with longer follow-up; this is not independent FDA CMC or safety clearance.

### Evidence favoring CRL / nonapproval

1. Global HARMONi **primary overall survival analysis did not meet statistical significance**: HR **0.79**, 95% CI **0.62–1.01**, p=**0.057** (median OS 16.8 vs 14.0 months).
2. According to Summit's Q3 2026 Form 10-Q, the **FDA explicitly said a statistically significant OS benefit was necessary** to support marketing authorization for this indication and that PFS data might be insufficient.
3. Updated OS analyses reported **nominal** p values after the primary test. The public documents do not establish that later tests controlled the original statistical multiplicity/alpha requirements. An unadjusted later p<0.05 is not automatically an accepted confirmatory OS endpoint.
4. Grade 3+ treatment-related adverse events: **50.0% vs 42.2%**; treatment-related discontinuations **7.3% vs 5.0%**; serious hemorrhagic events and cardiovascular/VEGF-related risk merit individual review.
5. Application-specific manufacturing/CMC, independent safety review, inspection status, complete statistical analysis plan and original FDA communications are not publicly available here. Unknown does **not** imply a negative finding.

### Decision rationale

Because the FDA's reported requirement directly targets the unachieved primary overall-survival significance test, the **research-only directional forecast favors CRL** as of the dated predecision evidence freeze. The positive PFS and improving OS estimates materially temper certainty. No numeric probability is justifiable without independently adjudicated statistical, CMC and historical calibration evidence.
