# ABBV / SKYRIZI (risankizumab-rzaa) — October 23, 2026 historical FDA action

## Exact regulatory cycle and date integrity

- Applicant: **AbbVie**, ticker **ABBV**.
- Product: **SKYRIZI (risankizumab-rzaa)**, an IL-23p19 antagonist.
- Exact application: **BLA 761105, efficacy supplement S-053** for a new **subcutaneous induction** regimen in adults with moderately to severely active **Crohn's disease**. Do not conflate this with pre-existing SKYRIZI approval for intravenous induction and subcutaneous maintenance, nor its other indications or supplemental applications.
- Pivotal trial: **AFFIRM (NCT06063967)**, Phase 3 randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind SC induction in adult Crohn's disease.
- **October 23, 2026** is the *FDA action date* and is listed as a PDUFA date by the third-party CatalystAlert October 2026 calendar. **The precise original regulatory target date is not independently confirmed by a dated predecision sponsor or FDA announcement**, so record October 23 as *tracker-listed goal / FDA action* with that uncertainty rather than claiming an independently verified scheduled goal.
- **Actual FDA decision:** **APPROVED October 23, 2026** for SC induction via on-body injector, as confirmed by AbbVie's dated same-day announcement and 2026 FDA label.
- **Retrospective manual three-layer direction:** **APPROVAL LEAN**, not independently calibrated, and not a production engine output.
- **Outcome was fully known before authoring**, so this is **NOT blind**, cannot be scored as a valid historical holdout or prospective success, and must not change accuracy pies, confidence, calibration or first frozen forecast histories.
- Dated predecision *reasoning* cutoff: **October 13, 2026**; the outcome source is used only to state the actual outcome after reconstruction.

## Level 1-style: phase-first clinical design and trial efficacy

- AFFIRM Phase 3, randomized, double-blind placebo-controlled induction trial, NCT06063967. AbbVie's March 2, 2026 results announced co-primary endpoint superiority for **clinical remission** and **endoscopic response** at week 12 versus placebo, with ranked secondaries positive.
- AbbVie's October 13, 2026 scientific disclosure expanded endoscopic results and stated the U.S. filing remained under FDA review.
- The product had already been FDA approved for Crohn's disease using IV induction and SC maintenance, so this review concerns a new administration regimen, not a new active molecule or a novel disease indication.

## Level 2-style: clinical benefit and safety

- A fully self-administered SC induction course offers the practical benefit of avoiding initial infusion visits. Study efficacy on clinical remission and endoscopic mucosal response supports clinical benefit, separate from route convenience.
- Safety in the predecision Phase 3 sponsor disclosures was consistent with the established risankizumab Crohn's profile with no new safety risks identified. This does not eliminate the need for full FDA adverse-event adjudication and inspection/CMC reconciliation.
- Primary study and sponsor findings were available before the approval, but the complete regulator review package and confidential manufacturing findings were not.

## Level 3-style: regulatory interpretation

- A positive, randomized active-route trial meeting both co-primary outcomes, plus years of prior product approval, supports an **APPROVAL LEAN** for the new subcutaneous induction regimen as of October 13, 2026.
- Unresolved risk: FDA could require additional device / human factors, manufacturing, labeling, immunogenicity or bridging data. **Unknown CMC information is a data gap, not evidence of an FDA deficiency**.
- No numerical approval probability is supported by independently validated calibration.
- The present analysis is fully outcome-aware; it is NOT an independent proof that Level 3 predicts approvals.

## True outcome and comparison

FDA authorized the new SC induction route on **October 23, 2026**. AbbVie's release reports week-12 trial data and the expanded on-body injector regimen. The qualitative retrospective **approval lean** matches the actual **approval**, but no true predictive success rate can be inferred.
