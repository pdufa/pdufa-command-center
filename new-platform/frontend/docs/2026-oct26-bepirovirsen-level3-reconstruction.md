## Three analytical layers, using only predecision sources

### Level 1 — Identity, study design and statistical evidence

- HBsAg 100–3000 IU/ml, adults with noncirrhotic CHB receiving stable nucleos(t)ide analogue (NA) therapy.
- Two replicating randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials. Bepirovirsen (300 mg weekly for 24 weeks) versus placebo alongside stable NA therapy.
- The primary endpoint was functional cure at week 72: sustained HBV DNA below the quantification limit and loss of HBsAg for at least 24 weeks after fixed-duration treatment, including planned NA withdrawal.
- **B-Well 1:** **127/650 (19.5%)** on active drug versus **0/328 (0%)** placebo met the primary endpoint.
- **B-Well 2:** **106/570 (18.6%)** versus **0/286 (0%)**. Both trials reported statistically significant improvements.
- Combined counts **233/1220 (19.1%) versus 0/614** are descriptive pooling and do not replace trial-specific prespecified testing.
- Two positive replicated phase 3 studies markedly strengthen causal efficacy evidence relative to an uncontrolled small study.

### Level 2 — Meaningful benefit and safety

- An HBV functional cure is a high-value endpoint given the otherwise often prolonged dependence on antiviral maintenance.
- The absolute response rate remains approximately one-fifth, so most treated patients did **not** achieve the definition of cure at week 72.
- In pooled reporting: serious adverse events **7% on treatment versus 4% placebo**; during the treatment period, grade 3 or higher events **16% versus 3%**, with ALT elevation an important contributor.
- Safety requires review of transient hepatic inflammation, clinical management, and baseline heterogeneity. Full CMC inspection and complete FDA safety reconciliation were not independently available as of the cutoff.
- Unknown CMC or undisclosed FDA review questions are **data gaps**, not assumed deficiencies.

### Level 3 — Regulatory fit and benefit-risk call

- **April 28, 2026:** FDA accepted the NDA for **priority review**, with **Breakthrough Therapy Designation**, reinforcing seriousness and potential meaningful advantage.
- The regulatory case rests on replicated Phase 3 functional-cure results versus no functional cures on placebo, relevant in a disease with ongoing antiviral dependence.
- Important downside: elevated grade 3 events; a minority of patients achieve functional cure; long-term generalizability and possible monitoring requirements.
- Without an actual review file, confirmatory agency correspondence, inspection status or independently reproduced analysis, there is no defensible numerical probability or claim of certainty.
